# Month 6: Consolidation

## What this month is about

This is the last month, and there's **no new material**. Over five months you've built a big set of chunks: storytelling connectors, questions, small talk, opinions, explanations, hypotheticals, "never stop" phrases, group and humour skills, phone language, meeting language, and answers to tough questions. Month 6 is about making all of that **automatic**, so it comes out without thinking, in long, unscripted and high-pressure conversations.

To do that without simply repeating old lessons, every week looks at what you already know from a **new angle**. Week 21 takes your conversations **deeper**: from small talk to real talk, feelings, support, goals and values. Week 22 **polishes how you sound**: stress, pauses, emotion, the trickiest American sounds and speed control. Week 23 puts everything **under stress**: job interviews, being put on the spot, negotiating, conflict and talking to strangers. Week 24 is the **final test**.

Why does this matter for "talking without thinking"? Because real conversations don't stay in one lesson. In ten minutes you might tell a story, give an opinion, react to a joke, handle a tough question and talk about something personal. This month mixes everything together, at full speed, so your brain learns to grab the right chunk for any moment.

Month 5 gave you work and complex topics, and the first moves for tough questions. Month 6 takes those first moves out of the office and into every part of life, and adds the two things that make the biggest difference to how natural you sound: **depth** and **delivery**.

The source plan's three recycling blocks are built in: Week 21 recycles Months 1 and 4 (stories, small talk, groups, humour), Week 22 recycles Months 2 and 3 (opinions, explaining, hypotheticals, talking on the spot) and Week 23 recycles Month 5 (work, complex topics, tough questions).

## Goals for the month

By the end of Month 6 you can…

- [ ] Move any conversation from small talk to a real, personal conversation, and talk about feelings, goals and values naturally.
- [ ] Use stress, pauses, emotion and speed to sound clear and expressive, and say the American R, "ee/i", "th" and V/W clearly.
- [ ] Answer job interview questions with clear STAR stories, and give a useful first sentence within 2 seconds when put on the spot.
- [ ] Negotiate politely, stay calm in a conflict, and start a conversation with a stranger.
- [ ] Hold a **30-minute unscripted conversation** without stopping.
- [ ] Hit the final target: umms and long pauses about **half** your Week 1 baseline, and **8+ different connectors** in 2 minutes.
- [ ] Send Claude a complete report to build your next six-month plan.

### Your stubborn ten

The source plan's main idea for Month 6 is this: **open your phrase log and pick the 10 phrases you still don't use naturally. Use each one at least twice in that day's speaking.** This month, that runs alongside every lesson:

1. On **Day 1 of each week**, in the Review block, choose your "stubborn ten": phrases from Months 1–5 that you know but still don't *use*.
2. Every night, try to use them in the speaking drill and live conversations. Put a tick next to a phrase each time you use it.
3. On **Day 6**, phrases with 2+ ticks go back into normal review. Phrases with fewer ticks stay on next week's list.
4. In the drills, follow the source plan's other rule too: **do the hardest situation first.**

## How your nights change this month

The time split is the same as Month 5, with the most speaking time of the whole programme:

| Block | Time | What you do this month |
|---|---|---|
| **Shadowing** | 15 min | Speak-along ([Shadowing](../toolkit/shadowing.md)) with long-form interviews, debates and street interviews. In Week 22 the shadowing *is* the lesson (pause-and-repeat for single sounds, then speak-along). In Week 24 you redo your Week 1 clips. |
| **Main skill** | 20 min | A short lesson from a new angle, plus exercises. In Week 24, rapid-fire tests. |
| **Speaking drill** | 70 min | See the table below. |
| **Review** | 15 min | Spaced review out loud: yesterday's, last week's and last month's phrases ([Phrase log](../toolkit/phrase-log.md)), plus your stubborn-ten ticks. |

| Day | Speaking drill (70 min) |
|---|---|
| **Days 1 and 3** | Mixed drills built around the lesson: role-plays, [3/2/1](../toolkit/drill-3-2-1.md) and the [random-topic drill](../toolkit/random-topic-drill.md) |
| **Days 2 and 5** | 40 min **live conversation** ([voice mode](../toolkit/voice-mode-with-claude.md) as backup) + 30 min drill |
| **Day 4** | **Voice mode with Claude**, with a ready-to-paste prompt |
| **Day 6** | Weekly check: re-record, count [the 3 numbers](../toolkit/the-3-numbers.md), skill check and **live conversation** |
| **Day 7** | Light day: 30 minutes of listening, then rest |

**Live conversations:** 3 a week (Days 2, 5 and 6), or more if you can.

## End-of-month check

The Month 6 check happens on [Week 24 Day 6](week-24-final-test/day-6-weekly-check.md), together with the final test of the whole programme. You'll follow the [monthly check procedure](../toolkit/monthly-check.md), using your final baseline recording from [Week 24 Day 5](week-24-final-test/day-5-final-recording-and-comparison.md): the same prompt as your Week 1 baseline, "Tell me about a day that didn't go as planned", with a different day.

**Month 6 targets** (compared with your Week 1 Day 1 baseline):

| Number | Target |
|---|---|
| Umms and uhhs | about **half** your baseline (about a 50% drop) |
| Pauses longer than 2 seconds | about **half** your baseline (about a 50% drop) |
| Different connectors in 2 minutes | **8 or more** |
| Also | a recorded **30-minute** unscripted conversation |

Then you'll write down the situations where you still had to think, and send everything to Claude to build your **next six-month plan**. When you're done, go back to the [programme home](../README.md).
