## Before you start

- Have everything in one place: your score sheets from Days 1–3, last night's comparison table and "still had to think" list, your 30-minute conversation analysis, your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md) and your [phrase log](../../toolkit/phrase-log.md).
- **Live conversation #3 is tonight.** If you can, have it with someone who heard your English earlier in the programme.
- This is the last night of the programme. Take your time with sections 6 and 7. They decide what happens next.

## 1. Shadowing (free choice, 10 min)

Choose your **favourite** clip from the whole six months: the one you enjoyed most, not the hardest. Speak along with it, twice, just for fun.

## 2. Re-record test (15 min)

Yesterday you redid the baseline story. Tonight's re-record checks that it wasn't a lucky day, with a different kind of prompt.

**No preparation.** Read the prompt, press record, start talking within 2 seconds, and keep going for **2 full minutes**.

> **Prompt:** "Tell me about the biggest change in your life this year. What was it, how did it happen, how do you feel about it now, and what advice would you give someone who's about to go through something similar?"

This mixes a story ([Week 1](../../month-01-foundations/week-01-storytelling/README.md)), feelings ([Week 21](../week-21-deep-conversations/README.md)), advice ([Week 21 Day 3](../week-21-deep-conversations/day-3-giving-advice-and-support.md)) and a hypothetical ([Week 10](../../month-03-spontaneity/week-10-hypotheticals/README.md)).

Count [the 3 numbers](../../toolkit/the-3-numbers.md) and add them to the **final results table** in the [Day 6 materials](exercises.md#day-6-materials).

## 4. Live conversation (45 min)

See [Live conversations](../../toolkit/live-conversations.md).

**Your mission: the victory lap.** Just enjoy a conversation. No checklist tonight. This is what all of this was for: talking with a real person, in English, without thinking about English.

If your partner has heard you before, ask them at the end: "Have you noticed any difference in my English since we first talked?" Write down exactly what they say.

Afterwards, write one sentence: "Tonight, I had to think about my English… (never / a few times / often)."

**Backup plan:** if the live conversation falls through, use [voice mode with Claude](../../toolkit/voice-mode-with-claude.md) for 30 minutes:

```
Let's have a relaxed 30-minute voice conversation, like two friends celebrating at the end of a long project. I just finished six months of nightly English speaking practice. Talk with me about anything: my plans, your (made-up) plans, travel, food, funny stories, big ideas. No corrections during the conversation. At the very end, tell me one thing that sounded completely natural and one thing to keep working on.
```

## 6. Monthly check (15 min)

Follow the [monthly check procedure](../../toolkit/monthly-check.md). Your **Month 6 check recording is last night's final baseline** ("Tell me about a day that didn't go as planned"), because it uses the same prompt as your Week 1 baseline and every monthly check. Tonight's re-record is a bonus check: if its numbers are close, last night wasn't a lucky day.

**Month 6 targets** (compared with your Week 1 Day 1 baseline):

| Target | Your result | Hit? |
|---|---|---|
| Umms about **half** your baseline (about a 50% drop) | | |
| Long pauses about **half** your baseline (about a 50% drop) | | |
| At least **8 different connectors** in 2 minutes | | |
| A 30-minute unscripted conversation, recorded | | |
| 3 live conversations a week through Month 6 | | |

**Compare with last month:** look at your [Month 5 check](../../month-05-work-and-complex-topics/week-20-thinking-on-your-feet/day-6-weekly-check.md) target (about a 45% drop). Did you keep going down, stay level, or go up a bit? A small rise in the final week is common (test nerves, harder prompts). Look at the **trend across all six months**, not one recording.

**If you hit the targets:** congratulations. You did what you set out to do. Your English has moved from "passive" to "active" in most everyday situations.

**If you didn't hit them all:** that's normal, and it's useful information. Look at what *did* change (speed, story quality, accent, confidence, the 30-minute conversation) and include it in your report. The next plan will focus on what's still slow. Don't just push on with the same plan. Adjust it.

## 7. The next 6 months (15 min)

Your original plan ends with this idea: **the next plan depends on what's still slow.** Tonight you have everything you need to decide.

### Step 1: Choose your direction (5 min)

Look at your "still had to think" list ([Exercise 5.4](exercises.md#day-5-exercises)) and your test scores. Which description fits you best?

| If this is you… | A good next 6 months could be… |
|---|---|
| People understand you, but your accent still gets comments, and Week 22 scored lowest | **Deep accent focus:** American pronunciation, intonation and connected speech, every night |
| You talk easily about everyday things, but you want more precise, advanced words and you're thinking about IELTS 7+ | **C1 vocabulary and IELTS 7+:** advanced chunks, academic and professional topics, exam-style speaking |
| Casual talk is fine, but presenting, meetings and audiences still make you freeze (Weeks 16, 17 and 20 scored lowest) | **Public speaking and presenting:** talks, demos, pitches, panels, leading meetings |
| Your numbers are still high in groups and fast conversations (Weeks 13–14 and the 30-minute conversation were hardest) | **Real-time listening and group fluency:** fast native speech, groups, humour, interruptions |
| You're not sure | Send the report and let Claude suggest a mix based on your weakest areas |

### Step 3: Until the new plan starts (5 min)

Don't stop completely while you wait. Skills you stop using fade fast. For the next 1–2 weeks, do this **maintenance routine** (about 30–45 minutes a night):

1. 10 minutes of speak-along with a clip you like ([Shadowing](../../toolkit/shadowing.md)).
2. One random topic, 2 minutes, recorded ([Random-topic drill](../../toolkit/random-topic-drill.md)).
3. 10 minutes of phrase review out loud ([Phrase log](../../toolkit/phrase-log.md)).
4. For every week that scored 1 in the final test, redo that week's Day 1 lesson (one per night).
5. Keep at least **2 live conversations a week**. That's the habit that matters most.
