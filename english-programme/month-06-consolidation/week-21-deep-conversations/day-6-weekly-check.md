# Week 21 · Day 6: Weekly check

**Tonight (2 hours):** Shadowing 15 min · Re-record test 15 min · Skill check 10 min · Live conversation 60 min · Review and report 20 min

## Before you start

- Have your recording app, your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md), your stubborn-ten page and last week's numbers (from the [Week 20 check](../../month-05-work-and-complex-topics/week-20-thinking-on-your-feet/day-6-weekly-check.md)) ready.
- **Live conversation #3 is tonight.** Book a full hour if you can: a meetup, a dinner with an English-speaking friend, a long call with a language partner.
- Read [the 3 numbers](../../toolkit/the-3-numbers.md) again if you need a reminder of how to count.

## 1. Shadowing (free choice, 15 min)

Go back to the clip from this week that felt **hardest** to speak along with. For most people that's the Day 2 sympathy clip (because the tone is so different from normal speaking) or the Day 5 values clip (because the pauses are hard to copy). Speak along with it 3 times. On the third time, record yourself and listen: does your voice sound warm, or are you rushing?

## 2. Re-record test (15 min)

**No preparation.** Read the prompt, press record, start talking straight away, and keep going for **2 full minutes**.

> **Prompt:** "Tell me about a person who changed your life in some way. Who are they, how did you meet, what did they do or say, and what did you learn from them that you still carry with you today?"

This prompt pulls together the whole week: a story (rung 2), feelings (rung 3) and values (rung 4).

Then listen back and count [the 3 numbers](../../toolkit/the-3-numbers.md):

1. Umms and uhhs
2. Pauses longer than 2 seconds
3. Different connectors (not just "and" and "so")

Also count one bonus number this week: **how many precise feeling words** did you use (drained, torn, relieved…)? Write all of them in your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md).

## 3. Skill check

Rate yourself honestly for each day. **3** = I can do it easily, without thinking. **2** = I can do it, but I have to think. **1** = I can't really do it yet.

| Day | I can… | Rating (1–3) | If you scored 1… |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Day 1: Small talk to real talk](day-1-from-small-talk-to-real-talk.md) | …move a conversation from facts to feelings in 3–4 natural steps, sharing first | | Redo Exercise 1.2 and one "climb the ladder" role-play from Day 1, Part A |
| [Day 2: Feelings](day-2-talking-about-feelings.md) | …describe a feeling precisely, with a size, and talk about mixed feelings | | Redo Exercise 2.2 out loud and do the Day 2 3/2/1 again |
| [Day 3: Advice and support](day-3-giving-advice-and-support.md) | …listen, validate and give soft advice, without "you should" | | Redo two role-plays from Exercise 3.3, focusing on validation |
| [Day 4: Goals and dreams](day-4-goals-and-dreams.md) | …talk about a goal with its why, steps and obstacle, and ask others about theirs | | Redo Exercise 4.1 and ask Claude in voice mode for 10 more dream questions |
| [Day 5: Values](day-5-values-and-beliefs.md) | …explain a value with a reason or story, and respond respectfully to a different one | | Redo Exercise 5.2 and the Day 5 3/2/1 |

Redo the 1-rated items on Day 7 (keep it to 30 minutes) or before Day 1 next week.

**Stubborn ten:** look at your ticks. Phrases with 2+ ticks: move them to your normal review. Phrases with 0–1 ticks: keep them for next week's stubborn ten.

## 4. Live conversation (60 min)

See [Live conversations](../../toolkit/live-conversations.md).

**Your mission: the deep-conversation challenge.** Have one conversation of at least 20 minutes that goes **beyond small talk**. You don't need to reach values. Reaching feelings (rung 3) with real, relaxed back-and-forth is a win.

Try these 5 things:

1. Answer "How are you?" with an honest, precise feeling word.
2. Use one question from the [Day 6 materials](exercises.md#day-6-materials) question set at a natural moment.
3. When they share something, validate it before you say anything else ("That makes total sense", "No wonder…").
4. Ask them about a goal or a dream, and follow up at least 3 times.
5. Use at least 3 of your stubborn ten.

If you're in a group (a meetup, a dinner), try to take one person aside for the deeper part. Deep conversations are easier one-on-one. Then use your group skills from [Week 13](../../month-04-real-life-pressure/week-13-group-conversations/README.md) for the rest.

**Backup plan:** if the live conversation falls through, use [voice mode with Claude](../../toolkit/voice-mode-with-claude.md) for 40 minutes with this prompt:

```
Let's do a 40-minute voice conversation. You're Casey, an old friend I haven't seen for a year. We meet for a long walk. Start with small talk and catching up, then let it go deeper: how we've really been, something hard that happened, our goals for next year, and what we've learned. Share your own made-up stories and feelings too. Use casual American English and short turns. At the very end, tell me: which bridge phrases I used, one moment where I could have validated your feelings better, and 3 corrections.
```

3. **Carry into next week:** Week 22 is about sounding natural: stress, pauses, emotion, sounds and speed. Tonight's re-record is a good recording to come back to. On Week 22 Day 3 (emotion in your voice), you'll listen to it again.
