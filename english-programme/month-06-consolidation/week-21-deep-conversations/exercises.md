# Week 21 exercises: Deep conversations
[Week 21 overview](README.md) · [Day 1](day-1-from-small-talk-to-real-talk.md) · [Day 2](day-2-talking-about-feelings.md) · [Day 3](day-3-giving-advice-and-support.md) · [Day 4](day-4-goals-and-dreams.md) · [Day 5](day-5-values-and-beliefs.md) · [Day 6](day-6-weekly-check.md)

**How to use these exercises:** everything is **out loud**. Reading silently doesn't count this month. When an exercise says "record", record it and listen back once. When you answer personal questions, you can use real answers or invent them: the language is what you're practising, not your private life. Model answers are in the answer key at the end of each day. Look at them only **after** you've said your own version.

## Day 3 exercises

### Exercise 3.1: Validate first

**Time:** 8 min · **Type:** out loud

A friend tells you each problem below. Respond out loud in **three steps**: (1) one follow-up question **or** a validation phrase, (2) the check "Do you want advice, or do you just need to vent?" (or a natural variation), and (3) imagine they say "advice": give one **soft** suggestion. Keep each answer under 30 seconds.

1. "My roommate never cleans the kitchen."
2. "I've got a job interview on Monday and I'm so nervous."
3. "My manager keeps changing the deadline."
4. "I think my best friend is mad at me, but I don't know why."
5. "I can't sleep. I keep thinking about work."
6. "My parents want me to move back home."
7. "I've been at this job for three years and I'm bored."
8. "I said something stupid in a meeting today."
9. "I wanna quit social media, but I can't."
10. "My dog's been sick all week."
11. "I keep spending way too much money on takeout."
12. "Nobody on my team gives me feedback."

### Exercise 3.2: Soften the advice

**Time:** 6 min · **Type:** out loud

Each piece of advice is too strong. Say a softer version, using a different phrase each time ("Have you thought about…?", "What if you…?", "If it were me, I'd probably…", "One thing that helped me was…", "For what it's worth…", "Maybe it's worth…").

1. "You must talk to your boss."
2. "You should break up with him."
3. "Don't worry about it."
4. "You need to go to bed earlier."
5. "You have to tell her the truth."
6. "Just quit."
7. "You should exercise more."
8. "Stop thinking about it."
9. "You must apologize."
10. "You should definitely take the job."

### Exercise 3.3: Support scripts

**Time:** 6 min · **Type:** out loud (gap-fill), then use in tonight's drill

**Part 1: Gap-fill.** Read the dialogue out loud, saying the missing phrase in each gap. Check the key.

> A: Hey, are you OK? You seem kinda quiet.
> B: Yeah, just… my team lead told me my project's being canceled.
> A: Oh no. (1) ______ What happened?
> B: Budget cuts. Six months of work, gone.
> A: Six months? (2) ______ you're upset. Anyone would be.
> B: I just feel like it was a waste of time.
> A: (3) ______, or do you just need to vent?
> B: Honestly… advice, I think.
> A: OK. (4) ______, I don't think it's a waste. You could show it in your portfolio. (5) ______ asking your lead if you can present what you learned to the team?
> B: Huh. That's actually a good idea.
> A: And (6) ______ I had a project canceled last year, and (7) ______ writing down everything I'd learned from it. It made it feel less pointless.
> B: Yeah. OK. Thanks.
> A: Of course. (8) ______ if you need to talk, OK?

**Part 2: Six situations for tonight's role-plays.** Say your friend's first line out loud, then respond. Use the full Listen → Validate → Advise → Support order. Each one: 3–4 minutes.

1. **Lost job:** "So… I got laid off today. They let go of like twenty people."
2. **Big presentation:** "I'm presenting to the CEO on Thursday and I honestly think I'm gonna throw up."
3. **Moving cities:** "I got offered a job in Seattle. It's amazing, but all my friends are here."
4. **Career doubt:** "I think I picked the wrong career. I'm thirty-two. Is it too late to change?"
5. **Friendship:** "My oldest friend hasn't replied to my messages in like a month."
6. **Can't sleep:** "My big interview is tomorrow at nine and it's midnight and I'm wide awake."

### Answer key

**Exercise 3.1: model answers for 1, 4 and 8**

1. "Ugh, that's so annoying. How long has it been like that? … Do you want advice, or do you just need to vent? … OK. What if you made a simple cleaning schedule together? Like, not in an angry way, just 'Hey, can we figure this out?'"
4. "Oh no, that's such a horrible feeling, not knowing. … Do you want ideas, or just to talk it out? … If it were me, I'd probably just ask her. Like, 'Hey, is everything OK with us?' It's scary, but at least you'd know."
8. "Oh no, what did you say? … Honestly, I bet nobody even noticed. Do you want advice, or just to vent? … For what it's worth, everyone says stupid stuff in meetings. One thing that helped me was making a joke about it the next day. It kinda takes the pressure off."

**Exercise 3.2: model answers**

1. "Have you thought about talking to your boss?"
2. "Do you think he's making you happy? If it were me, I'd probably think about whether it's working."
3. "I know it's hard, but for what it's worth, I think it's gonna be OK."
4. "Maybe it's worth trying to go to bed a little earlier?"
5. "What if you just told her the truth? It might be easier than you think."
6. "Have you thought about looking for something else?"
7. "One thing that helped me was going for a run after work. Even just twenty minutes."
8. "I know it's hard to stop thinking about it. What if you wrote it all down and then put it away for tonight?"
9. "Maybe it's worth apologizing? Even a short message."
10. "For what it's worth, if it were me, I'd probably take it."

**Exercise 3.3, Part 1:** (1) That's rough. / That sucks. (2) No wonder (3) Do you want advice (4) For what it's worth (5) What if you tried / Have you thought about (6) honestly, / I get it, (7) one thing that helped me was (8) I'm here

3. "Working from home is lazy."

8. "Big cities are better than small towns, no question."

10. "Being on time is a sign of respect."
