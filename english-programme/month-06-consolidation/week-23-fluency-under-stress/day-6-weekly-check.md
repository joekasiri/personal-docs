# Week 23 · Day 6: Weekly check
[Programme home](../../README.md) › [Month 6](../README.md) › [Week 23](README.md) · [← Previous: Talking to strangers](day-5-talking-to-strangers.md) · [Next: Week 24 · Review of Months 1 and 2 →](../week-24-final-test/day-1-review-months-1-and-2.md)

**Tonight (2 hours):** Shadowing 15 min · Re-record test 15 min · Skill check 10 min · Live conversation 60 min · Review and report 20 min

## Before you start

- Have your recording app, the [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md), your stubborn-ten page and last week's numbers ready.
- Choose tonight's **stress mission** from the [Day 6 materials](exercises.md#day-6-materials) **before** you start, so you can't talk yourself out of it.
- This is the last weekly check before the final test week. Take it seriously, but don't panic: next week is mostly a celebration of how far you've come.

## 1. Shadowing (free choice, 15 min)

Go back to the **fastest** clip from this week (usually the Day 2 talk-show clip or the Day 3 debate clip) and speak along at full speed, 3 times. Then do your 5-minute sound routine from Week 22. Fast speech under pressure is exactly what this week trained.

## 2. Re-record test (15 min)

**No preparation.** Read the prompt, press record, start talking within 2 seconds, and keep going for **2 full minutes**.

> **Prompt:** "Tell me about a time you had to deal with a difficult person or a stressful situation. What happened, what did you do, how did it end, and what would you do differently now?"

This mixes a STAR story (Day 1), staying calm under pressure (Day 4) and a lesson. To make it harder (the point of this week), **stand up and imagine you're in a job interview** while you record.

Then count [the 3 numbers](../../toolkit/the-3-numbers.md): umms, pauses over 2 seconds, different connectors. Also note: **how many seconds before your first word?** Write everything in your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md).

## 3. Skill check

Rate yourself honestly. **3** = I can do it under pressure. **2** = I can do it when I'm calm. **1** = not yet.

| Day | I can… | Rating (1–3) | If you scored 1… |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Day 1: Interviews](day-1-job-interview-answers.md) | …answer a behavioral question with S-T-A-R + lesson in under 2 minutes | | Redo Exercise 1.2 and answer 3 behavioral questions from Exercise 1.1 with a timer |
| [Day 2: On the spot](day-2-being-put-on-the-spot.md) | …start any answer within 2 seconds and give a short version | | Redo the hot-seat drill (Exercise 2.1) with a timer |
| [Day 3: Negotiating](day-3-negotiating-and-persuading.md) | …ask for what I want, trade with "if", and close a deal | | Redo two scenarios from Exercise 3.2, playing a tougher other side |
| [Day 4: Conflict](day-4-handling-conflict-calmly.md) | …acknowledge, slow down, explain and solve when someone is upset | | Redo Exercise 4.1 in a low, slow voice, then one voice-mode conflict |
| [Day 5: Strangers](day-5-talking-to-strangers.md) | …start a conversation with a stranger and keep it going for 5 minutes | | Do the stranger backup role-play from Day 5, then try one real opener this week |

**Stubborn ten:** move phrases with 2+ ticks to your normal review. Any that are still stubborn after three weeks: write them on a sticky note and put it somewhere you'll see every day next week.

## 4. Live conversation (60 min)

See [Live conversations](../../toolkit/live-conversations.md).

**Your mission: a stress mission.** Choose **one** mission from the [Day 6 materials](exercises.md#day-6-materials) (you chose it before you started, right?). It should feel a bit scary. That's the point. Then have a normal conversation for the rest of the hour.

Whatever mission you choose, try these 4 things:

1. Start every answer within 2 seconds.
2. When you feel your heart beating fast, use a brake phrase and slow down ([Week 22 Day 5](../week-22-sounding-natural/day-5-controlling-your-speed.md)).
3. Use at least one phrase from each day of this week.
4. Use at least 3 of your stubborn ten.

Afterwards, write down: the mission, what happened, how nervous you felt before (1–10) and after (1–10).

**Backup plan:** if the live conversation falls through, use [voice mode with Claude](../../toolkit/voice-mode-with-claude.md) for 40 minutes:

```
Let's do a 40-minute "stress test" voice session. Switch between these situations every 8 minutes, without warning me: (1) a job interview for a product designer role with a tough interviewer, (2) a dinner party where someone suddenly asks me to tell a story to everyone, (3) a negotiation where I'm trying to get a lower rent from my landlord, (4) a coworker who is annoyed with me about a missed deadline, (5) a stranger at a conference who starts chatting with me. Make each situation realistic and a little stressful. At the end, tell me which situation I handled best and worst, and give me 5 corrections.
```

## 5. Review and report (20 min)

1. **Write your numbers** in the [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md).
2. **Send Claude your report** in a normal text chat:

```

Re-record (2 min, standing, "a difficult person or situation"):
- Umms: __ (last week: __, baseline: __)
- Long pauses: __ (last week: __, baseline: __)
- Different connectors: __ (last week: __)
- Seconds before my first word: __
Skill check (1-3): Day 1 interviews __, Day 2 on the spot __, Day 3 negotiating __, Day 4 conflict __, Day 5 strangers __
Stranger score from Day 5: __ conversations started, longest __ minutes
Stress mission: __ . Nervous before: __/10, after: __/10
Live conversations this week: __ of 3
Stubborn ten: __ of 10 used at least twice. Still stubborn after 3 weeks: __

Next week is my final test. Please tell me (1) what my numbers show compared with my baseline, (2) what to focus on during the review tests, and (3) anything I should do on Day 7 to be ready.
```

3. **Carry into next week:** Week 24 is the **final test**. Days 1–3 test everything from Months 1–6, Day 4 is a 30-minute conversation with Claude, Day 5 is your final baseline recording, and Day 6 is the final check and the plan for your next six months. Before Day 1, find these and put them in one folder: your **Week 1 Day 1 baseline** recording, your Week 1 shadowing recordings (if you kept them), and all your monthly check numbers.
