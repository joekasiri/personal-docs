# Week 23: Fluency under stress
[Programme home](../../README.md) › [Month 6: Consolidation](../README.md) · [← Week 22](../week-22-sounding-natural/README.md) · [Week 24 →](../week-24-final-test/README.md)

## This week's goal

Everyone sounds fluent when they're relaxed. The real test of "talking without thinking" is what happens when your heart is beating fast: a job interview, being suddenly asked to speak in front of people, negotiating something you care about, an argument, or walking up to a stranger. Stress shrinks your vocabulary, speeds you up, flattens your voice and brings back the "umm"s. This week you train for exactly those moments, at full speed, so that your ready-made chunks come out **even when you're nervous**. This is the hardest week of the programme on purpose: it's the last week before the final test.

## How this builds on last week

In [Week 22](../week-22-sounding-natural/README.md) you learned to control your stress, pauses, emotion, sounds and speed when calm. This week you keep that control under pressure: a brake phrase and a silent pause are exactly what you need when an interviewer surprises you.

The source plan says Week 23 recycles Month 5: work, complex topics and tough questions. You won't re-learn those lessons. Instead, you'll put them under new kinds of stress:

- your success and failure stories from [Week 18 Day 5](../../month-05-work-and-complex-topics/week-18-explaining-your-work/day-5-success-and-failure-stories.md) and your 60-second answers from [Week 8 Day 1](../../month-02-speed/week-08-automatic-answers/day-1-sixty-second-answers.md) become **interview answers** (Day 1)
- your first moves for unexpected questions from [Week 20 Day 2](../../month-05-work-and-complex-topics/week-20-thinking-on-your-feet/day-2-unexpected-questions.md) move from the office to **social** situations (Day 2)
- your polite push-back from [Week 17 Day 3](../../month-05-work-and-complex-topics/week-17-meetings/day-3-pushing-back-politely.md) and defending ideas from [Week 20 Day 3](../../month-05-work-and-complex-topics/week-20-thinking-on-your-feet/day-3-defending-an-idea.md) become **negotiation** (Day 3)
- your difficult conversations from [Week 20 Day 4](../../month-05-work-and-complex-topics/week-20-thinking-on-your-feet/day-4-difficult-conversations.md) become **staying calm when emotions rise** (Day 4)
- your small talk ([Week 3](../../month-01-foundations/week-03-small-talk/README.md)) and meeting new people ([Week 12 Day 3](../../month-03-spontaneity/week-12-role-plays/day-3-meeting-new-people.md)) become **cold approaches to strangers** (Day 5)

## Before you start

- Finish the [Week 22 weekly check](../week-22-sounding-natural/day-6-weekly-check.md) and add Claude's 5-minute sound routine to your shadowing.
- Pick your **stubborn ten** on Day 1.
- Choose **two real work stories** you know well: one success, one failure or mistake. If you already wrote them down in Week 18, find those notes. You'll need them on Day 1.
- Plan your 3 live conversations (Days 2, 5 and 6). **Day 5 is special**: you'll talk to strangers. A meetup, a co-working space, a community event, a class, a café with shared tables or a dog park are all good. Pick a place now. See [Live conversations](../../toolkit/live-conversations.md).
- A word about nerves: this week is supposed to feel uncomfortable. Feeling nervous isn't a sign that you're failing. It's the training.

## Day 7: light day

**30 minutes only.** Find a real, long-form job interview or a "mock interview" video where a real candidate answers questions (not a scripted example). Search for "real mock interview product designer", "mock interview full length" or "interview practice real candidate". Listen while you walk.

Your one task: **be the hiring manager.** After each answer, say out loud (quietly is fine) one thing the candidate did well and one thing you'd do differently. Then think: would you hire them? Notice how much of a good answer is *calm structure*, not perfect English. Write one phrase you'd steal into your phrase log. Then stop and rest. Next week is the final test, so tonight's rest matters.

## By the end of this week you can…

- [ ] Answer common job interview questions with clear, story-based answers in under 2 minutes.
- [ ] Give a useful first sentence within 2 seconds when someone puts you on the spot.
- [ ] Negotiate politely for what you want, and find a middle ground.
- [ ] Stay calm and clear when someone is angry with you, and calm the situation down.
- [ ] Start and keep up a conversation with a stranger for 5 minutes.
- [ ] Keep your sounds, pauses and speed under control even when you're nervous.
