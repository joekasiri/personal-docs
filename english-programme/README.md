# English Speaking Programme

**Goal:** talk without thinking. Fast, relaxed casual conversation in front of people, with an American-style accent.
**Starting level:** B2 (CEFR), around IELTS 6
**Time:** 2 hours a night, 6 nights a week, plus a light Day 7
**Length:** 6 months: 6 months × 4 weeks = 24 weeks, 144 lessons

**New here? → [Getting started](toolkit/getting-started.md)**, then **[Week 1 · Day 1](month-01-foundations/week-01-storytelling/day-1-storytelling-connectors.md)**.

## Why this programme works

At home you use **passive** vocabulary: words you recognise and can reach slowly. Under pressure, your brain only has time for **active** vocabulary: phrases you've said out loud so many times they come out automatically. So the main job is not learning lots of new words. It's **saying the words you already know, out loud, under time pressure, again and again.**

"Talking without thinking" comes from three things, and every month trains all three:

1. **Chunks, not single words.** Native speakers don't build sentences word by word. They pull out ready-made pieces like "the thing is…" or "I was gonna say…". The more chunks you own, the less you have to build.
2. **Speed and pressure.** Every month gets a bit faster, less prepared and more public, so your brain stops translating and starts grabbing.
3. **Never stopping.** When you don't know a word, you go *around* it ("it's like a…, the thing you use to…") instead of freezing. That habit matters more than knowing every word.

## The 6 months at a glance

| Month | Focus | By the end of the month you can… |
|---|---|---|
| [1: Foundations](month-01-foundations/README.md) | Stories, questions, small talk, everyday vocabulary | tell a practised story using real connectors, with fewer pauses |
| [2: Speed](month-02-speed/README.md) | Better stories, opinions, explaining, automatic answers | tell stories and give opinions at close to normal speed |
| [3: Spontaneity](month-03-spontaneity/README.md) | No preparation: on-the-spot talk, hypotheticals, never stopping, role-plays | talk for 2 minutes on a random topic with zero prep, and keep going when you're missing a word |
| [4: Real-life pressure](month-04-real-life-pressure/README.md) | Groups, humour, calls, speaking in front of people | hold your own in a group chat and on the phone |
| [5: Work and complex topics](month-05-work-and-complex-topics/README.md) | Meetings, explaining your work, big topics, thinking on your feet | run your part of a meeting and handle questions you didn't expect |
| [6: Consolidation](month-06-consolidation/README.md) | Deep conversations, sounding natural, fluency under stress, final test | speak without translating in your head, and prove it against your Day 1 recording |

## How the folders work

```
english-programme/
├── README.md                     ← you are here
├── toolkit/                      ← shared drills, setup and tracking
└── month-NN-<focus>/
    ├── README.md                 ← month overview: goals, prerequisites, the 4 weeks
    └── week-NN-<topic>/          ← weeks are numbered 01–24 across the programme
        ├── README.md             ← week overview: goal, prerequisites, the 7 days
        ├── day-1-<topic>.md      ← one lesson = one full night (all 4 blocks)
        ├── …
        ├── day-5-<topic>.md
        ├── day-6-weekly-check.md ← re-record, self-check, live conversation (+ monthly check in weeks 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24)
        └── exercises.md          ← every exercise for the week, with full content and answer keys
```

**Every lesson links to** the previous and next lesson, its week overview and its exercises. So once you start, you can just follow the "Next →" links all the way to Week 24.
