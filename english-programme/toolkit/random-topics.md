# Random topic cards

[Programme home](../README.md) · [Toolkit](README.md) · How to use: [Random-topic drill](random-topic-drill.md)

120 topics in three levels. Pick a number without looking, then talk straight away.

- **Level 1:** everyday things you know about
- **Level 2:** experiences and opinions
- **Level 3:** ideas, arguments and "what if"
