# Shadowing

[Programme home](../README.md) · [Toolkit](README.md)

Shadowing means **copying a native speaker out loud**: their sounds, rhythm, speed and melody, like an actor learning a role. It's the fastest way to an American-style accent, and it trains your mouth to produce fast English without thinking.

## Choosing clips

**Good clips:**
- A casual American speaker *chatting*, not presenting, reading or acting
- One or two speakers, clear audio, no music over the voices
- Subtitles or a transcript, especially in month 1
- 1–3 minutes long. You'll replay them many times.

Each week's lessons tell you what *kind* of clip fits that night's sound or skill.

## Method 1: Pause-and-repeat (month 1)

1. **Listen** to the whole clip once, just for meaning.
2. **Play 1–2 sentences, then pause.**
3. **Repeat out loud**, copying *exactly*: the speed, where the voice goes up and down, which words are long and which are squashed, even the "uh"s and laughs.
4. Replay the same sentence and repeat again, 3–5 times, until it sounds close.
5. Move to the next 1–2 sentences.
6. At the end, **record yourself** doing the whole clip and compare with the original.

**Focus on one thing at a time.** Every lesson gives you tonight's focus (like the flap T, or linking). Listen for that and exaggerate it.

## Method 2: Speak-along (month 2 onwards)

1. Do pause-and-repeat once on the clip to learn it.
2. Now **play the clip and speak at the same time as the speaker**, without pausing. Stay with them, even a tiny bit behind.
3. First with subtitles, then without.
4. Do the same clip 3–5 times.

It's hard at first and you will fall behind. That's the point. Speak-along forces your mouth to work at native speed, which is exactly the speed you need in real conversation.

## Marking a transcript

If your clip has a transcript, copy a few lines and mark them up:

- **CAPITALS** for stressed words: "I was SO not ready for that"
- **‿** for words that link together: "pick‿it‿up"
- **↗ ↘** for the voice going up or down: "You're coming↗? Great↘."
- **/** for where the speaker pauses: "So basically / I get there / and nobody's home."

## Tips

- **Exaggerate.** If it feels too American, it's probably about right.
- **Copy the feeling,** not just the words. Bored, excited, sarcastic, tired.
- **Mouth first, meaning second.** In shadowing you're training your mouth, not your vocabulary.
- **Keep your clips.** In [Week 24](../month-06-consolidation/week-24-final-test/README.md) you redo your Week 1 clips and hear how far you've come.
