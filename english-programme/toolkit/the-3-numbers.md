# The 3 numbers

[Programme home](../README.md) · [Toolkit](README.md)

Three numbers tell you, honestly, whether your speaking is getting more automatic. You count them on a **2-minute recording**: your baseline, every Day 6 re-record, and the last round of your drills.

## The numbers

| # | What you count | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Fillers** | Every "umm", "uhh", "err" and empty sound | How often your brain is searching for words |
| **2. Long pauses** | Every silence **longer than 2 seconds** | How often your brain gets stuck completely |
| **3. Connectors** | How many **different** connectors you used. "So", "and" and "but" don't count. | How natural and joined-up your speech is |

**Connectors that count:** so basically, the thing is, anyway, and then out of nowhere, turns out, which was weird because, I was like, he/she goes, long story short, the funny part is, at that point, in the end, plus any others from your lessons (honestly, I mean, speaking of, that reminds me, on the other hand…). Each one counts **once**, however often you use it.

**Natural fillers are fine.** "Like", "you know" and "I mean" are things native speakers say, so don't count them as fillers. After [Week 3](../month-01-foundations/week-03-small-talk/README.md) they should be *replacing* your umms. That's progress.

## How to count

1. Play the recording. Have a paper and pen.
2. **First listen:** a tally mark for every umm/uhh (number 1).
3. **Second listen:** a tally mark for every silence over 2 seconds (number 2). Count "one-Mississippi, two-Mississippi". If you reach "three", it counts.
4. **Third listen:** write down each different connector (number 3).
5. Write the three numbers in your [nightly log](nightly-log.md).

It takes about 8 minutes. That's part of the drill time, not extra.

## Your baseline

On [Week 1 Day 1](../month-01-foundations/week-01-storytelling/day-1-storytelling-connectors.md) you record **2 minutes, unprepared**, before you learn anything. That's your baseline. Every later number is compared with it. **Keep that recording forever.**

## What "good" looks like

These are targets compared with *your* baseline, not anyone else's:

| End of month | Fillers + long pauses | Different connectors in 2 min |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | about 15% fewer | 4 or more |
| 2 | about 25% fewer | 5 or more |
| 3 | about 33% fewer | 6 or more |
| 4 | about 40% fewer | 6 or more |
| 5 | about 45% fewer | 7 or more |
| 6 | about half | 8 or more |

**Example:** baseline 24 umms + 8 long pauses = 32. The month 3 target is about 21.

## Don't cheat yourself

- Re-records on Day 6 must be **unprepared**. Pick the prompt and start.
- Don't re-record because the first try was bad. The first try *is* the result.
- Bad weeks happen, especially in months 3–4 when the drills get harder. Look at the trend across a month, not one night.
