# The random-topic drill

[Programme home](../README.md) · [Toolkit](README.md) · Used from [Month 3](../month-03-spontaneity/README.md) · Topics: [Random topic cards](random-topics.md)

## What it is

You pick a topic **without looking** and start talking **immediately**, for 1–2 minutes. No preparation, no notes, no stopping. This is the closest you can get, alone, to real conversation, where nobody gives you time to plan.

The 4/3/2 and 3/2/1 drills trained *speed with practised content*. This drill trains *speed with no content ready*. That's the real goal.

## How to do it

1. Pick a level from [Random topic cards](random-topics.md):
   - Weeks 9–10: Level 1
   - Weeks 11–12: Levels 1–2
   - Month 4: Levels 2–3
   - Months 5–6: Level 3, or your week's own topic list
2. Pick a number without looking (roll dice, use a random number app, or close your eyes and point).
3. **Start recording and start talking within 2 seconds.** Your first words can be a starter (see below).
4. Talk for **1 minute** (weeks 9–10) or **2 minutes** (week 11 onwards).
5. Next topic, straight away. Do **5–10 topics** in a row.
6. Pick the topic you found hardest and do **one [3/2/1](drill-3-2-1.md) round** on it.
7. Listen to one recording and count your [3 numbers](the-3-numbers.md).

## Starters that buy 2 seconds (and sound natural)

- "Oh, that's a good one. So…"
- "Hmm, OK. Honestly, the first thing that comes to mind is…"
- "Huh. I've never really thought about that, but…"
- "OK so, funny story actually…"
- "I mean, it depends, but for me…"

## A simple shape when your mind is empty

If nothing comes, use this shape. It always works:

1. **Reaction:** "Coffee? Oh, I could talk about coffee all day."
2. **Me:** what's your connection to it? "I'm kind of addicted, to be honest."
3. **Story or example:** one time it mattered. "Like, last week I forgot to have one before a meeting and…"
4. **Opinion:** "I honestly think…"
5. **Hand over:** "What about you? Are you a coffee person?"

## Rules

- **No skipping topics.** "I don't know anything about this" is a great start: "I don't know much about volcanoes, but I'm guessing…"
- **No silence over 2 seconds.** Use the "never stop" phrases from [Week 11](../month-03-spontaneity/week-11-never-stopping/README.md).
- **Being wrong is fine.** Nobody's checking your facts. They're checking that you keep talking.
