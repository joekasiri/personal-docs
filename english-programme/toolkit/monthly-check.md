# Monthly check

[Programme home](../README.md) · [Toolkit](README.md)

At the end of every month (Day 6 of weeks 4, 8, 12, 16, 20 and 24) you add a monthly check to your weekly check. It takes about 20 minutes and tells you whether the plan is working **for you**.

## The procedure

1. **Record** an unprepared 2-minute talk. Use the prompt from your Day 6 lesson.
2. **Count** your [3 numbers](the-3-numbers.md).
3. **Listen to your Week 1 baseline**, then last month's check recording. Notice the difference in speed, pauses and how natural you sound.
4. **Fill in the table** below.
5. **Phrase log:** count phrases with 3 ticks, and pick next month's 10 "must use" phrases ([how](phrase-log.md#once-a-month)).
6. **Send Claude your results** with the message at the bottom of this page.

## Targets (compared with your baseline)

| End of month | Fillers + long pauses | Different connectors in 2 min | Also check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | about 15% fewer | 4+ | You can tell a practised story without stopping |
| 2 | about 25% fewer | 5+ | You give an opinion and a reason in under a minute |
| 3 | about 33% fewer | 6+ | 2 minutes on a random topic with zero prep |
| 4 | about 40% fewer | 6+ | You joined in at least one group conversation |
| 5 | about 45% fewer | 7+ | You handled an unexpected question at work |
| 6 | about half | 8+ | You don't translate in your head in casual chat |

**How to work out "vs baseline":** (baseline total − this month's total) ÷ baseline total × 100.
Example: baseline 32, now 24 → (32 − 24) ÷ 32 × 100 = **25% fewer**.

## If you didn't hit the target

Don't panic, and **don't restart the month**. Do this instead:

| Problem | What to change next month |
|---|---|
| Lots of fillers | Spend 5 extra minutes a night on the time-buyer phrases from [Week 3 Day 4](../month-01-foundations/week-03-small-talk/day-4-time-buyers.md) |
| Lots of long pauses | Do 3 extra random topics every night ([random-topic drill](random-topic-drill.md)), even in months 1–2 |
| Few connectors | Before every drill, choose 3 connectors you haven't used this week |
| Felt stuck in live conversations | Add one extra live conversation or voice-mode session a week |
| Not enough time | Cut the main skill block by 10 minutes and add it to the speaking drill. Speaking always comes first. |
