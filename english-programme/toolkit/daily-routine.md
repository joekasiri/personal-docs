# Daily routine

[Programme home](../README.md) · [Toolkit](README.md)

Every night has the same four blocks, in the same order. As the months go on there's **less studying and more speaking**, because that's how "knowing" turns into "talking without thinking".

## The four blocks

| Block | What you do | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Shadowing** | Copy a native speaker's sounds, rhythm and melody out loud ([how](shadowing.md)) | Trains your mouth and your accent. It also warms you up so you don't start the night "cold". |
| **2. Main skill** | Tonight's lesson: explanation, key phrases, examples, then exercises out loud | Gives you new chunks (ready-made phrases) to use |
| **3. Speaking drill** | Use tonight's phrases under time pressure: 4/3/2, 3/2/1, random topics, voice mode or a live conversation | This is where phrases become automatic |
| **4. Review** | Log new phrases and review old ones out loud ([how](phrase-log.md)), fill in the [nightly log](nightly-log.md) | Stops you forgetting. Old phrases come back until they're yours. |

## Speaking drills by month

| Month | Days 1–5 | Day 4 | Day 6 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [4/3/2](drill-4-3-2.md) with tonight's story prompt | Voice mode with Claude | Re-record + live conversation |
| 2 | [3/2/1](drill-3-2-1.md) with tonight's prompt | Voice mode with Claude | Re-record + live conversation |
| 3–4 | Days 1, 5: [random-topic drill](random-topic-drill.md) + one 3/2/1 round. Day 2: voice mode. Day 3: 30 min drill + 30 min live conversation | Voice mode with Claude | Re-record + live conversation |
| 5–6 | Days 1, 3: mixed drills (random topic, 3/2/1, role-play). Days 2, 5: 40 min live conversation + 30 min drill | Voice mode with Claude | Re-record + live conversation |

If you can't get a live conversation on a given day, use [voice mode with Claude](voice-mode-with-claude.md) instead. Don't skip the speaking.

## Review by month

| Month | Review block |
|---|---|
| 1 | Log 5 new phrases. Reread yesterday's phrases. |
| 2 | Log 5 new phrases. Say yesterday's phrases **out loud from memory**, then check. |
| 3–6 | Log 5 new phrases. Spaced review out loud: yesterday's, last week's, last month's ([schedule](phrase-log.md#the-review-schedule)). |

## The weekly rhythm

| Day | What's special |
|---|---|
| **Days 1–5** | A new lesson each night. Use it straight away in that night's speaking drill. |
| **Day 4** | The speaking drill is always voice mode with Claude |
| **Day 6** | Weekly check: an unprepared 2-minute re-record, your [3 numbers](the-3-numbers.md), a skill self-check and a live conversation |
| **Day 7** | **Light day, 30 minutes only.** Listen to a casual podcast while walking (each week's overview gives you a specific listening task). Then rest. Your brain locks in what you practised. |

## Missed a night?

- **Missed one night:** do the next lesson as normal. Add that night's exercises to Day 7.
- **Missed 3+ nights:** go back to the start of that week. Speed matters less than not leaving gaps.
- **Every night feels too easy:** do the exercises faster and record more. Don't skip ahead.
- **Every night feels too hard:** that's normal in months 3–4. Keep going and use the "never stop" phrases from [Week 11](../month-03-spontaneity/week-11-never-stopping/README.md) as soon as you get there.
