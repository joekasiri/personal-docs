# Getting started

[Programme home](../README.md) · [Toolkit](README.md) · [Start Week 1 →](../month-01-foundations/week-01-storytelling/day-1-storytelling-connectors.md)

Do this setup **once, before Week 1 Day 1**. It takes about an hour. After that, every night is just "open the lesson and follow it".

## 1. Choose your time and place

- **Time:** the same 2 hours every night, 6 nights a week. Day 7 is a light 30-minute day.
- **Place:** somewhere you can **speak out loud** without feeling watched. This programme only works out loud. Reading the lessons silently does almost nothing for speaking.
- **Headphones** with a microphone, for shadowing and voice mode.

## 2. Set up recording

You record yourself almost every night.

- Use your phone's built-in voice recorder app. Anything that saves audio files works.
- Name each recording like this: `W01-D1-baseline`, `W01-D2-432-run3`. Week, day, then what it is.
- Keep every recording in one folder. **Never delete your Day 1 baseline.** You compare against it at the end of every month.
- Learn how to count your numbers before Day 1: [The 3 numbers](the-3-numbers.md).

## 3. Make your phrase log

Your phrase log is the most important thing you build in this programme. It's where phrases go from "I've seen this" to "I say this without thinking".

- Use a notes app or a physical notebook, whichever you'll actually open every night.
- Set it up the way [Phrase log](phrase-log.md) shows: date, phrase, your own example sentence, times used.

## 4. Copy the nightly log

Copy the table from [Nightly log](nightly-log.md) into a spreadsheet, notes app or notebook. It takes 2 minutes a night to fill in.

## 5. Find your shadowing sources

Read [Shadowing](shadowing.md), then save **5 clips** you like before you start:

- Casual American speakers *chatting*, not presenting or reading.
- Good search terms on YouTube or podcast apps: "storytime vlog", "two friends podcast", "casual chat podcast", "day in my life vlog", "comedy podcast clips".
- Clips with subtitles or a transcript make the first weeks easier.

## 6. Set up voice mode with Claude

Every Day 4 is a voice conversation with Claude.

- Open the Claude app on your phone and find voice mode.
- Do a 3-minute test: say "Hi, can you hear me? Let's just chat for a minute."
- Read [Voice mode with Claude](voice-mode-with-claude.md) and save the standard prompt somewhere easy to copy.

## 7. Line up live conversations

Day 6 of every week includes **one real conversation with a real person**. From month 3 you need two a week, and from month 5 three. Read [Live conversations](live-conversations.md) and, before Week 1 ends, have at least one of these ready:

- a colleague you can have a 15-minute coffee chat with in English
- a language-exchange app account
- a local English-speaking meetup
- an online tutor for conversation practice

## 8. Know your routine

Read [Daily routine](daily-routine.md) once. It explains the four blocks of every night and how they change over the 6 months. Then glance at [All-day habits](all-day-habits.md).
