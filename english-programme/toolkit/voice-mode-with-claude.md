# Voice mode with Claude

[Programme home](../README.md) · [Toolkit](README.md)

Every **Day 4** your speaking drill is a voice conversation with Claude. It's also your **backup** whenever a live conversation falls through. Claude never gets tired, never judges, and can correct you, but only if you set the session up well.

## Setting up

1. Open the Claude app and start a **new chat** each time.
2. **Type** (paste) your setup prompt first. Use the standard prompt below, or the one in tonight's lesson.
3. Switch to **voice mode** and start talking.
4. Record the session with your phone's recorder if you can. It's useful for counting your numbers later.

## The standard setup prompt

Paste this, then add tonight's topic from your lesson at the end:

```
I'm practising casual spoken American English. My level is B2 and my goal is to speak fast and relaxed without thinking.

Please:
- Talk to me like a friendly American friend, casually, using contractions and everyday phrases.
- Keep your turns short (1–3 sentences) so I do most of the talking.
- Ask me follow-up questions so I have to keep going.
- Don't correct me during the conversation. Just remember my mistakes.
- When I say "feedback", stop and give me: my 5 most important mistakes with the correct version, 3 phrases I could have used to sound more natural, and one thing I did well.

Tonight's topic and focus:
```

## During the session

- **Do most of the talking.** If Claude talks more than you, say "Ask me more questions."
- **Use tonight's phrases on purpose.** Have the key-phrase list open and tick them off as you use them.
- **Never go silent.** Use a time-buyer ("Let me think…", "That's a good question…") instead.
- **Ask questions back.** Real conversation goes both ways.

## Ending the session

1. Say **"feedback"**.
2. Write down the corrections in your [phrase log](phrase-log.md). These are the most valuable phrases you'll ever log, because they're *your* mistakes.
3. Repeat each correct version **out loud 3 times**.

## Useful things to say to Claude mid-session

| You want | Say |
|---|---|
| A harder conversation | "Speak faster and use more slang." |
| Easier | "Slow down a little and use simpler words." |
| Pressure | "Interrupt me sometimes, like a real person would." |
| A role-play | "Let's role-play. You're a barista and I'm ordering." |
| Practice for a specific phrase | "Ask me questions that make me use [phrase]." |
| A quick check | "Did that sentence sound natural?" |

## Reporting your weekly numbers

On Day 6, send Claude your numbers in a normal text chat:

```
Week [N] numbers. Baseline: [umms] umms, [pauses] long pauses, [connectors] connectors.
This week: [umms] umms, [pauses] long pauses, [connectors] connectors.
What I found hardest this week: [...]
Can you suggest 3 things to focus on next week?
```
