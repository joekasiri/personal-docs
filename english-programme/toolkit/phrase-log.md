# Phrase log

[Programme home](../README.md) · [Toolkit](README.md)

Your phrase log is where phrases go from **passive** (you recognise them) to **active** (they come out by themselves). You add 5 phrases a night, and the review schedule brings them back until they're automatic.

## What to log

Log **chunks, not single words**:

- ❌ "suspense"
- ✅ "And you'll never guess what happened next."
- ❌ "agree"
- ✅ "Yeah, I'm with you on that."

**Best sources, in this order:**
1. Corrections from Claude's voice-mode feedback (your *own* mistakes)
2. Things you wanted to say today but couldn't ([all-day habits](all-day-habits.md#4-the-how-would-an-american-say-that-game))
3. Tonight's key phrases that felt hardest
4. Phrases you heard in a clip or conversation

## How to log

| Date | Phrase | My own sentence | Lesson | Used ✓ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 Jan | Turns out… | Turns out the restaurant was closed on Mondays. | W01 D1 | ✓✓ |
| 12 Jan | That's rough. | Oh man, that's rough. Are you OK? | W01 D2 | ✓ |
| 13 Jan | What I need is… | What I need is a really long vacation. | W02 D3 | |

- **My own sentence:** always about *your* life. That's what makes it stick.
- **Used ✓:** add a tick every time you use it in a real conversation or voice session. **Three ticks = it's yours.** Highlight it and stop reviewing it.

## The review schedule

Reviewing is **out loud**: you look at the phrase, cover your sentence, and say a *new* sentence with the phrase.

| Month | Each night's review (15 min) |
|---|---|
| 1 | Log 5 new phrases. Reread yesterday's phrases and say each one in a sentence. |
| 2 | Log 5 new phrases. Cover the phrase column, look at "my sentence", and **say the phrase from memory**. Then check. |
| 3–6 | Log 5 new phrases. Say out loud: **yesterday's** phrases, phrases from **exactly one week ago**, and phrases from **exactly one month ago**. |

The 1-day / 1-week / 1-month gaps are deliberate: you review just as you're about to forget, which is when memory gets strongest.

## Once a month

On the last Day 6 of each month:
1. Count how many phrases have 3 ticks.
2. Find the 10 oldest phrases with **no ticks**. Make them next month's "must use" list.
3. In [month 6](../month-06-consolidation/README.md) your whole main-skill time can draw on this list.
