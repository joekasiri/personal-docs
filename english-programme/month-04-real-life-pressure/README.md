# Month 4: Real-life pressure
[Programme home](../README.md) · [← Month 3: Spontaneity](../month-03-spontaneity/README.md) · [Month 5: Work and complex topics →](../month-05-work-and-complex-topics/README.md)

## What this month is about

Up to now, most of your speaking has been "clean": one listener, a quiet room, a clear question, and nobody watching. Real life is messier. Three friends talk at the same time. Someone makes a sarcastic joke and you are not sure if they mean it. The phone line cuts out in the middle of an address. Ten people turn to look at you because it is your turn to say who you are. This month trains you for exactly those moments.

Pressure is where "talking without thinking" is really tested. In a quiet room your brain has time to translate. In a noisy group, on a bad phone line or in front of people, it does not. If a phrase is not automatic, it will not come out. So this month gives you small, ready-made chunks for each pressure situation ("Oh, that reminds me…", "Sorry, you cut out for a sec", "Guilty as charged", "I'll keep this short") and makes you say them under time pressure until they come out by themselves.

The month has four weeks: **groups** (getting a turn, keeping it, giving it away), **humour and banter** (sarcasm, exaggeration, timing, teasing and reacting), **calls** (phone and video, where you have no faces or hands to help you), and **speaking in front of people** (introductions, stories, toasts and questions while others listen).

It builds directly on Month 3. In Month 3 you stopped preparing and learnt to keep going on any topic, to paraphrase, to correct yourself and to improvise role-plays. This month you keep all of that and add *other people* and *noise*. You will not re-learn those skills. You will use them in harder places, and the lessons link back when they recycle them.

## Goals for the month

By the end of Month 4 you can:

- Jump into a group conversation of 3+ people at least three times in one evening, using a natural entry phrase instead of waiting for silence.
- Hand the conversation to a quiet person by name, keep your turn politely when someone cuts in, and build on someone else's idea with "Yeah, and…".
- Recognise sarcasm from tone, use light sarcasm and exaggeration about situations, tell a short funny story with a pause before the punchline, and react to jokes in under two seconds.
- Make plans, leave a clear voicemail, handle a bad connection and call a business (booking, checking, complaining) without losing the details: names spelt, numbers and times confirmed.
- Introduce yourself to a group in 30 seconds, tell a story to 3+ people, give a 1–2 minute toast or update, and answer a question while others listen.
- Recover from a mistake in front of people in one short sentence and carry on.
- Cut your "umm"s and long pauses by about **40%** compared with your Week 1 baseline.

## Before you start

- You should have finished [Month 3: Spontaneity](../month-03-spontaneity/README.md), including the month 3 check in [Week 12 · Day 6](../month-03-spontaneity/week-12-role-plays/day-6-weekly-check.md). Have your month 3 numbers ready to compare.
- Reread the toolkit pages you will use every night: [daily routine](../toolkit/daily-routine.md), [random-topic drill](../toolkit/random-topic-drill.md), [3/2/1 drill](../toolkit/drill-3-2-1.md), [shadowing](../toolkit/shadowing.md) (speak-along method), [voice mode with Claude](../toolkit/voice-mode-with-claude.md), [live conversations](../toolkit/live-conversations.md) and [phrase log](../toolkit/phrase-log.md).
- **Find a group.** This month at least one live conversation each week should have 3 or more people. Before Week 13 starts, look for: a language exchange evening with group tables, a board-game café night, a hobby meetup (running club, photography walk, book club), a team lunch or after-work drinks, or an online group call (many language apps have group rooms). Put two dates in your calendar now.
- **Find two "call people".** For Week 15 you need one or two friends or language partners who are happy to take a 10-minute phone or video call and to swap English voice notes with you. Ask them this week so it is not a surprise.
- Get a **standing spot** at home where you can speak at full volume for Week 16 (a room where you will not feel shy about being loud).

## How your nights change this month

The time split is the same as Month 3: less studying, more speaking.

| Block | Minutes | What you do this month |
|---|---|---|
| Shadowing | 15 | **Speak-along** with whole clips (no pausing). The clips change with the week: multi-host podcasts, comedy chat, phone-style audio, speeches and toasts. See [shadowing](../toolkit/shadowing.md). |
| Main skill | 30 | One pressure skill per night (Days 1–5), with key phrases, examples and exercises. |
| Speaking drill | 60 | **Days 1 and 5:** [random-topic drill](../toolkit/random-topic-drill.md) with this week's twist + one [3/2/1](../toolkit/drill-3-2-1.md) round. **Days 2 and 4:** voice mode with Claude ([how](../toolkit/voice-mode-with-claude.md)). **Day 3:** 30 min drill + 30 min live conversation #1 (voice mode as backup). **Day 6:** re-record test + live conversation #2. |
| Review | 15 | Spaced review **out loud**: yesterday's phrases, last week's phrases, and last month's phrases ([phrase log](../toolkit/phrase-log.md)). |

**Live conversations:** 2 per week (Day 3 and Day 6). Try to make **one of them a group of 3+ people** every week. In Week 15 at least one should be a real phone or video call.

**Day 7** is always the light day: 30 minutes of listening, then rest. Each week's README gives you a specific listening task.

## The 4 weeks

| Week | Focus | You'll be able to… |
|---|---|---|
| [Week 13: Group conversations](week-13-group-conversations/README.md) | Getting, keeping and giving turns with 3+ people | jump in, hand over, hold your turn, ask people to repeat and build on others' ideas without freezing |
| [Week 14: Humour and banter](week-14-humour-and-banter/README.md) | Sarcasm, exaggeration, timing, teasing, reactions | understand and use friendly humour, tell a funny story with timing and laugh along naturally |
| [Week 15: Phone and video calls](week-15-calls/README.md) | Talking with no body language to help | make plans by phone, leave voicemails, survive bad connections, call businesses and send casual voice notes |
| [Week 16: Speaking in front of people](week-16-speaking-in-front-of-people/README.md) | Introductions, stories, toasts and questions with an audience | speak to a group with a strong voice, pause for effect, answer in public and recover from mistakes |

## End-of-month check

The month check happens in [Week 16 · Day 6](week-16-speaking-in-front-of-people/day-6-weekly-check.md). You will:

1. Re-record a 2-minute unprepared talk **standing up, at "audience" volume**, and count your 3 numbers ([how to count](../toolkit/the-3-numbers.md)).
2. Compare them with your Week 1 baseline and your month 3 recording, using the [monthly check procedure](../toolkit/monthly-check.md).
3. Have one last live conversation, ideally in a group, where you use at least one skill from each of the four weeks.

**Targets for Month 4 (compared with your Week 1 baseline):**

| Number | Target |
|---|---|
| "Umm"s and "uhh"s | down about 40% |
| Pauses longer than 2 seconds | down about 40% |
| Different connectors in 2 minutes | at least 6 (on the way to 8 by Month 6) |
| Pressure test | You took part in at least 4 group conversations and 1 real phone call this month |

If your numbers are not going down, do not just push on into Month 5. Send them to Claude (the Day 6 report message has a template) and adjust the plan together.
