**How to use these exercises:** this week, **don't look at anyone and don't use your hands**. Do the speaking parts with your eyes closed or facing a wall, ideally holding your phone to your ear. Read the other person's lines first, then close your eyes and answer. All names, numbers and addresses are invented (555 numbers are fake). ### Exercise 3.2: The choppy call
**Time:** 12 min · **Type:** out loud, eyes closed for your answers

Read each line. Where you see **…(crackle)…**, stop and say what you'd say: name the problem, say what you last heard, ask for the missing part. Then imagine the answer and continue.

**Call A: Noor, from the train**
1. "Hey! So the dinner is …(crackle)… next Thursday."
2. "And it's at the restaurant on …(crackle)…"
3. "It's for, like, …(crackle)… people, maybe more."
4. "Can you get there by seven-fif…(crackle)…?"
5. *(silence for five seconds)*
6. "…hello? Did you get all that?"

**Call B: video call with your colleague Ben**
7. *(Ben's mouth is moving, but there's no sound.)*
8. "Okay, so, I think the …(crackle)… is the main problem."
9. *(Ben's face hasn't moved for ten seconds.)*
10. *(You both start talking at the same time.)*
11. "Can you send me the file by …(crackle)…?"
12. *(It keeps freezing and cutting out.)*

### Exercise 3.3: Fix-it phrases, rapid fire
**Time:** 10 min · **Type:** out loud, 2-second limit

Say the right phrase for each problem within two seconds. Go through the list three times, faster each time.

1. Their voice comes in pieces.
2. They're talking but you hear nothing (and see their mouth move).
3. Their video stopped moving.
4. You moved to a better room and want to check.
5. You missed one word: a time.
6. You missed a whole sentence.
7. You both talked at the same time.
8. There's a long delay.
9. It's impossible to continue.
10. Your video might be slowing the call.
11. You want to check your own sound at the start.
12. You want to move the details to text.

### Exercise 5.2: One-take voice notes
**Time:** 10 min · **Type:** record, one take only

Record each voice note in **one take** (45–90 seconds). If you make a mistake, fix it with "I mean…" and keep going. Never stop and restart.

1. Tell a friend about the best thing you ate this week.
2. Invite a friend to something next weekend (give a day, time and place).
3. Tell a friend a small annoying thing that happened, with sarcasm.
4. Recommend a show, podcast or app, and say why.
5. Ask a friend for advice about a decision (a trip, a purchase, a job).
6. Tell a friend you're sorry you missed their call, and suggest a time to talk.
7. Share a funny moment from your day (pause before the punchline!).
8. Wish a friend good luck before their exam or interview.

Checklist after each: opening? one or two topics? an ask? a closing? one take?
