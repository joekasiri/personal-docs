**Tonight (2 hours):** Shadowing 15 min · Re-record test 15 min · Skill check 15 min · Live conversation 60 min · Review and report 15 min

## Before you start

- Live conversation #2: tonight it should be a **phone or video call** (or a group video call). If you can, make it with someone new, or make one real call to a business (see the mission).
- [Nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md) open with last week's numbers and your baseline.
- Recording app and timer. [The 3 numbers](../../toolkit/the-3-numbers.md).

## 1. Shadowing (free choice, 15 min)

Redo the **hardest clip of the week**: probably the bad-connection call (Day 3) or the customer-service call (Day 4). Speak along twice. Then do one minute of the Day 3 "fifteen/fifty" sentences and one minute of spelling your practice name and email with "as in". Method: [shadowing](../../toolkit/shadowing.md).

## 2. Re-record test (15 min)

**Prompt (unprepared, 2 minutes), recorded as if it's a phone call, eyes closed:**

> "You're calling a friend who moved to another city. Tell them what's been happening in your life recently, suggest a day and time for a longer video call next week, and give them one recommendation (a show, a place, an app)."

1. Read the prompt once. Close your eyes. Start recording immediately with a call opening ("Hey! It's me…").
2. Talk for 2 minutes. Include a read-back of the plan and a closing.
3. Count your 3 numbers ([how](../../toolkit/the-3-numbers.md)): umms/uhhs, pauses over 2 seconds, different connectors.
4. Write them in your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md).
5. Bonus check: were the day and time clear enough to write down?

## 4. Live conversation (60 min)

**Mission: a real call in English, start to finish.** Use the checklist in [Day 6 materials](exercises.md#day-6-materials).

Choose **one or both**:

- **Option A (friend call, 20–40 min):** a phone or video call with a friend, colleague or language partner.
- **Option B (real business call, 5–10 min):** call a real business in English if you live somewhere you can (a restaurant to ask about opening hours or book a table, a shop to check if they have something, a gym to ask about classes). Use your real details only if you really want to book something.

Try to:

1. **Open** naturally and check it's a good time.
2. **Make or confirm a plan** with a read-back ("So just to confirm…").
3. **Spell something** (your name, a street, a website) with "as in".
4. **Check one number** (fifteen/fifty, a time, a price).
5. **Handle one connection issue** if it happens, or check "Can you hear me okay?" at the start.
6. **Close** with a next step: "I'll text you the address. Talk soon!"

**Backup plan:** a 40-minute voice session with Claude. Paste:

```
Let's do a phone-call test. Give me 4 calls in a row, eyes closed, natural speed:
1) my friend RAY calls to make weekend plans (lots of times and places),
2) I call a gym to ask about prices and book a trial class (you're the receptionist; ask me to spell my name),
3) a bad video call with my colleague PRIYA (freeze twice, go on mute once),
4) I leave a voicemail for my landlord about a broken heater, then he calls me back.
My practice identity: Alex Moreno, 555-0192, 48 Pine Street, Apartment 3B.
After all 4, rate me 1-3 on: opening/closing, confirming details, numbers, spelling,
handling bad connections. Give examples and up to 5 mistakes.
```

See [live conversations](../../toolkit/live-conversations.md).
