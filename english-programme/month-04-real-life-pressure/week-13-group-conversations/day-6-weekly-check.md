# Week 13 · Day 6: Weekly check

**Tonight (2 hours):** Shadowing 15 min · Re-record test 15 min · Skill check 15 min · Live conversation 60 min · Review and report 15 min

## Before you start

- Your **group conversation** (3+ people) should be booked for tonight. If the group meets on another evening this week, move tonight's blocks to that day.
- Have your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md) open with last week's numbers (Week 12 Day 6) and your Week 1 baseline.
- Recording app and timer. Read [the 3 numbers](../../toolkit/the-3-numbers.md) if you need a reminder of how to count.

## 1. Shadowing (free choice, 15 min)

Go back to the **hardest clip of the week**: probably the one with the most overlapping voices (Day 3 or Day 4). Speak along with one host for 5 minutes. Then play it again and be the "extra host": make back-channels and say a jump-in phrase out loud at every landing point you hear. Finish with one more speak-along. Method: [shadowing](../../toolkit/shadowing.md).

## 2. Re-record test (15 min)

**Prompt (unprepared, 2 minutes):**

> "Describe the last time you were in a group of three or more people: a dinner, a meeting, a party or a trip. Who was there, what did everyone talk about, and how did you take part? Was it easy or hard to get a word in?"

1. Read the prompt once. Start the recording **immediately**. No notes.
2. Talk for 2 minutes without stopping.
3. Listen back and count your 3 numbers ([how](../../toolkit/the-3-numbers.md)):
   - "umm"s and "uhh"s
   - pauses longer than 2 seconds
   - different connectors
4. Write them in your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md) next to last week's.
5. Bonus count: how many of this week's phrases did you use? (Reported jump-ins like "and I was like, oh, that reminds me…" count.)

## 3. Skill check

Rate yourself honestly: **1** = I can't yet, **2** = I can with effort, **3** = it's automatic.

| Day | Skill | "I can…" | Rating (1–3) | If you scored 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Jumping in](day-1-jumping-in.md) | take a turn at a landing point with an entry chunk, and connect it to what was said | | Redo [Exercise 1.3](exercises.md#day-1-exercises) twice, faster, then shadow a multi-host clip and say the jump-ins out loud |
| 2 | [Handing over](day-2-handing-over.md) | end my turn clearly and pass it by name, with a hook | | Redo [Exercise 2.2](exercises.md#day-2-exercises) and run the Day 2 voice prompt for 15 min |
| 3 | [Holding your turn](day-3-holding-your-turn.md) | keep my turn with a smile and return to my point after an interruption | | Redo the Day 3 interruption drill with 2 stories |
| 4 | [Asking people to repeat](day-4-asking-people-to-repeat.md) | ask for a repeat in 2–4 words and echo the part I missed | | Redo [Exercise 4.1](exercises.md#day-4-exercises), then 10 min of the Day 4 voice prompt |
| 5 | [Building on others' ideas](day-5-building-on-others-ideas.md) | reply to an idea with "Yeah, and…" + something new, in five ways | | Redo [Exercise 5.1](exercises.md#day-5-exercises) with a 3-second limit |

Any skill with a 1: spend 10 minutes on it on Day 7 or before Week 14 Day 1. Any skill with a 2: use it on purpose in tonight's live conversation.

## 4. Live conversation (60 min)

**Mission: be an active member of a real group (3+ people).** Use the mission card in [Day 6 materials](exercises.md#day-6-materials) if you want something to keep in your pocket.

Try to:

1. **Jump in at least 3 times**, each time with a different entry chunk ("Oh, that reminds me…", "Can I just add something?", "Oh, speaking of…").
2. **Hand over at least twice**, once to the quietest person, by name, with a hook.
3. **Ask for a repeat or explanation at least once**, specifically ("Wait, who's…?", "You went where?").
4. **Build on someone's idea at least twice** with "Yeah, and…" or "Building on what [name] said…".
5. If someone cuts you off, **hold your turn once** with a smile, or let it go and come back with "Okay, so, as I was saying…".

Afterwards, write down: how many people, how many times you spoke, which of the five you did.

**Backup plan (if the group falls through):** do a 40-minute voice session with Claude playing three people. Paste this:

```
Group conversation practice. Play THREE people at a board-game night: JESS (loud, funny, interrupts),
OMAR (asks questions), and LIN (quiet, only talks when invited). Say each name before their line.
Make it fast and a bit chaotic, with inside jokes and overlapping chat.
I need to: jump in, hand over to Lin, hold my turn when Jess interrupts,
ask people to repeat, and build on others' ideas.
No corrections until I say "feedback". Then score me 1-3 on each of those five skills
with examples, and list up to 5 mistakes.
```

See [live conversations](../../toolkit/live-conversations.md) for nerves and preparation tips. Even with the backup, try to book a real group for Week 14.

3. **Carry into next week:** keep jumping in and handing over in every conversation. Next week adds jokes, so your group skills will get a workout: banter is fast, and you will need to jump in at the right moment.
