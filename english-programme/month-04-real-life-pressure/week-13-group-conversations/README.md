# Week 13: Group conversations
[Programme home](../../README.md) › [Month 4: Real-life pressure](../README.md) · [← Week 12](../../month-03-spontaneity/week-12-role-plays/README.md) · [Week 14 →](../week-14-humour-and-banter/README.md)

## This week's goal

One-to-one, the other person waits for you. In a group of three or more, nobody waits. Turns are short, people overlap, and the conversation moves on in seconds. Many B2 speakers go quiet in groups, not because they have nothing to say, but because they never find the "door" into the conversation. This week you learn the five moves that make groups work: **jumping in**, **handing over**, **holding your turn**, **asking people to repeat** and **building on other people's ideas**. Each move comes with short chunks you will drill until they come out without thinking.

## How this builds on last week

In [Week 12](../../month-03-spontaneity/week-12-role-plays/README.md) you improvised role-plays: ordering, making plans, meeting new people, asking for favours and arguing a side. Those were mostly one-to-one. This week you take that same improvising and put it in a room with two or three other voices. You will also recycle older tools: "Speaking of…" from [Week 3 · Day 5](../../month-01-foundations/week-03-small-talk/day-5-changing-topic-and-ending.md), echo questions from [Week 3 · Day 2](../../month-01-foundations/week-03-small-talk/day-2-echo-questions-and-showing-interest.md), question tags from [Week 2 · Day 4](../../month-01-foundations/week-02-questions/day-4-question-tags-and-short-answers.md) and "Where was I?" from [Week 11 · Day 4](../../month-03-spontaneity/week-11-never-stopping/day-4-staying-on-track.md).

## Day 7: light day

**30 minutes, then rest.** Put on a multi-host podcast (search "three hosts chat podcast" or "friends roundtable podcast") while you walk, cook or tidy. Your only task: **count the jump-ins.** Every time a speaker cuts in or takes a turn, notice the first two or three words they use ("Oh, and…", "Wait—", "Yeah, but…"). At the end, write down the three entry chunks you heard most often in your phrase log. No recording, no drills. Rest is part of the plan: your brain locks in the week while you sleep.

## By the end of this week you can…

- [ ] Spot a "landing point" in a group conversation and jump in with an entry phrase in under a second.
- [ ] Hand the conversation to a specific person, including a quiet one, by name.
- [ ] Keep your turn with a friendly "Hang on, let me finish" and get back to your point after an interruption.
- [ ] Ask someone to repeat or explain in a natural way, saying exactly which part you missed.
- [ ] Add to someone else's idea with "Yeah, and…" instead of only agreeing.
- [ ] Take part in a real group conversation of 3+ people and speak at least five times.
