**Tonight (2 hours):** Shadowing 10 min · Re-record test 15 min · Skill check 10 min · Live conversation 60 min · Review and report 10 min · Monthly check 15 min

This is the last night of Month 4. Tonight's re-record is also your **Month 4 recording**.

## Before you start

- Live conversation #2 booked, ideally a **group of 3+** where you might introduce yourself, tell a story or say a few words to everyone (a meetup, a dinner, a club, a group call).
- [Nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md) open with: your **Week 1 baseline** numbers, your **Month 3** numbers (from [Week 12 · Day 6](../../month-03-spontaneity/week-12-role-plays/day-6-weekly-check.md)), and this month's weekly numbers (Weeks 13–15).
- [Monthly check procedure](../../toolkit/monthly-check.md) open.
- Standing spot, phone recording. [The 3 numbers](../../toolkit/the-3-numbers.md).

## 1. Shadowing (free choice, 10 min)

Redo the **hardest clip of the week**: probably the toast (Day 3) or the audience Q&A (Day 4). Speak along standing up, projecting, copying the pauses. Then say your 30-second introduction from [Day 1](day-1-introducing-yourself-to-a-group.md) once at full volume. Method: [shadowing](../../toolkit/shadowing.md).

## 2. Re-record test (15 min)

**Prompt (unprepared, 2 minutes), standing up, at "audience" volume, as if 10 people are listening:**

> "You've been asked to say a few words at a meetup about your last four months: what you've been working on in your life, one moment that was hard, one moment you're proud of, and what's next."

1. Read the prompt once. Stand up. Start recording **immediately**. No notes.
2. Talk for 2 minutes. Start with "Hi everyone —". End with a clear final line.
3. If you make a mistake, recover like [Day 5](day-5-recovering-from-mistakes.md) and keep going.
4. Count your 3 numbers ([how](../../toolkit/the-3-numbers.md)): umms/uhhs, pauses over 2 seconds, different connectors. Note: a *planned pause for effect* (with a purpose, before a key line) is not a "long pause". A *stuck* silence is.
5. Write them in your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md). You'll use them in section 6.

## 4. Live conversation (60 min)

**Mission: the Month 4 final mission.** In a real group (3+ people), use at least **one skill from each week** of this month. Use the mission card in [Day 6 materials](exercises.md#day-6-materials).

Try to:

1. **Week 16 (audience):** introduce yourself to the group, or say a few words to everyone (a mini toast, an update, a story to the whole table). Project and pause.
2. **Week 13 (groups):** jump in at least twice and hand over to a quiet person once.
3. **Week 14 (humour):** react quickly to jokes, and use one sarcastic line or exaggeration about a situation.
4. **Week 15 (calls):** before or after, make a short call or send a voice note to someone in the group to make or confirm plans (read back the details).
5. **Week 16 (questions + recovery):** answer at least one question while others listen (PASH), and if you slip, recover in one sentence.

**Backup plan:** a 45-minute voice session with Claude. Paste:

```
This is my Month 4 final test. Play a group of 4 people at a community meetup:
HOST (organised), PAT (funny, teases a bit), MINA (quiet), LUIS (asks lots of questions).
Say each name before their line. Please include, in this order:
1) the host asks everyone to introduce themselves (I go third),
2) a fast group chat with jokes and sarcasm where I need to jump in and hand over to Mina,
3) Luis asks me questions in front of everyone, including one I can't answer,
4) the host asks me to give a short toast for Pat's birthday,
5) at the end, "call" me as Pat on a bad line to confirm plans for next week (cut out twice, mix up 15/50).
No corrections until I say "feedback". Then rate me 1-3 on: groups, humour, calls, speaking to an audience,
recovering from mistakes, with examples, and list up to 5 mistakes.
```

See [live conversations](../../toolkit/live-conversations.md).

## 6. Monthly check (15 min)

Follow the [monthly check procedure](../../toolkit/monthly-check.md). Tonight's re-record (section 2) is your Month 4 recording.

**1. Compare your numbers.** Fill in this table:

| | Week 1 baseline | Month 3 (Week 12) | Month 4 (tonight) | Change vs baseline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| "Umm"s / "uhh"s | | | | % |
| Pauses > 2 seconds | | | | % |
| Different connectors | | | | |

**Month 4 targets (vs baseline):** umms and long pauses **down about 40%**; **6+ different connectors** in 2 minutes; you spoke in at least **4 group conversations** and made at least **1 real phone or video call** this month.

To get the percentage: (baseline − tonight) ÷ baseline × 100. Example: baseline 20 umms, tonight 12 → (20 − 12) ÷ 20 × 100 = 40% down.

**2. Listen back.** Play your Week 1 baseline, then tonight's recording. Write three sentences: what sounds different? (Speed? Voice? Confidence? Connectors?)

**3. Month 4 pressure review.** Tick what you did this month:

- [ ] Took part in a group conversation of 3+ people (how many times: __ )
- [ ] Told a funny story with a pause before the punchline to real people
- [ ] Made a real phone or video call in English
- [ ] Left a voicemail or sent voice notes in English
- [ ] Introduced myself or said a few words to a group
- [ ] Gave a toast or a group update

**4. If you missed the targets**, don't just push on into Month 5. Look at which number is stuck. Too many umms → do 10 extra minutes of [time-buyers](../../month-01-foundations/week-03-small-talk/day-4-time-buyers.md) and [speak-along shadowing](../../toolkit/shadowing.md) daily. Too many long pauses → more [random-topic drills](../../toolkit/random-topic-drill.md) with a strict "never stop" rule. Too few connectors → review the [storytelling connectors](../../month-01-foundations/week-01-storytelling/day-1-storytelling-connectors.md) and use 3 on purpose in every drill. Then tell Claude.
