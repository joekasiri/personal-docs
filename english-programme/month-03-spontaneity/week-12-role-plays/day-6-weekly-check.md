## Before you start

- Tonight is the weekly check for Week 12 **and** the **Month 3 check**. It's the end of your "no preparation" month.
- Find these numbers before you start (from your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md)):
  - your **baseline** from [Week 1 Day 1](../../month-01-foundations/week-01-storytelling/day-1-storytelling-connectors.md)
  - your **Month 1 check** from [Week 4 Day 6](../../month-01-foundations/week-04-vocabulary/day-6-weekly-check.md)
  - your **Month 2 check** from [Week 8 Day 6](../../month-02-speed/week-08-automatic-answers/day-6-weekly-check.md)
- Live conversation #2 of the week should be booked. If not, the backup is voice mode (below).
- Have the [Day 6 materials](exercises.md#day-6-materials) ready, but **don't read the topic cards yet**.
- Read [The 3 numbers](../../toolkit/the-3-numbers.md) again if you need a reminder of how to count.

## 1. Shadowing (free choice, 10 min)

Redo the clip that was hardest this week. For most people that's the **confident and firm** debate clip from [Day 5](day-5-arguing-the-other-side.md) or the **excited vs unsure** clip from [Day 2](day-2-making-plans.md). Speak along with the whole clip twice, no pausing. On the second try, exaggerate the emotion — make polite *more* polite, excited *more* excited. Method: [Shadowing](../../toolkit/shadowing.md).

## 2. Re-record test (15 min)

This week's test is a **random-topic talk**, because "2 minutes on a random topic with zero prep" is the Month 3 goal.

1. Open the [Day 6 materials](exercises.md#day-6-materials). There are 10 topic cards you haven't used before. Cover them, then pick one number between 1 and 10 without looking (or ask someone to pick for you).
2. Read the card **once**, start recording, and start talking **within 2 seconds**.
3. Talk for **2 full minutes**. Don't stop the recording if you get stuck. Use your Week 11 tools: paraphrase, self-correct, "I'm not sure, but…", "Where was I?", "So basically…".
4. Stop at 2:00 exactly.
5. Listen back and count your [3 numbers](../../toolkit/the-3-numbers.md):
   - **Umms / uhhs:** ____
   - **Pauses longer than 2 seconds:** ____
   - **Different connectors** (not "and" or "so"): ____
6. Write them in your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md). Keep this recording: it's your Month 3 check recording too.

Also answer yes or no: **Did you talk for the full 2 minutes without stopping for more than 5 seconds?** ____

## 4. Live conversation (60 min)

This is **live conversation #2** of the week. See [Live conversations](../../toolkit/live-conversations.md).

**Mission: turn real life into role-plays.** During one conversation (or one outing), try to do these real things in English:

1. **Make a real plan** with your conversation partner: suggest a next session or a meet-up, agree on a time, and lock it in ("Does… work for you?", "Let's say…").
2. **Ask a small favour**: "Any chance you could recommend a podcast / correct one sentence for me?" — and use an exit door.
3. **Play devil's advocate once**: when your partner gives an opinion, say "OK, let me play devil's advocate…" and argue the other side for 30 seconds. Then drop the role and laugh about it.
4. **Introduce yourself with a hook** if it's a new person, and use their name twice.
5. **Bonus (real world):** before or after the conversation, order something at a café in English and ask for one change ("Can I get that with oat milk?").

**Backup plan (if your partner cancels): voice mode with Claude.** Paste this:

```text
Let's do a 45-minute mixed role-play to test my week. I'm a B2 English learner. Play these scenes one after another, without warning me what's coming, and without correcting me during the scenes:
1. A waiter who brings me the wrong order and is a bit defensive.
2. A friend who wants to make plans but keeps changing the day.
3. A stranger at a party who has something surprising in common with me.
4. A coworker I need a big favour from, who is very busy.
5. A friend who states a strong opinion — I have to argue the opposite side.
Keep your turns short and casual, American-style. After all five, give me: my best moment, my 5 most useful corrections, and the one scene I should practise again.
```

After the conversation, write down 3 things that went well and 1 moment where you froze.

## 6. Monthly check (15 min)

Follow the full procedure in [Monthly check](../../toolkit/monthly-check.md). Use tonight's 2-minute random-topic recording.

**Month 3 targets (compared with your Week 1 Day 1 baseline):**

- **Umms and long pauses down about 33%** (a third). To find your target, multiply your baseline number by 0.67. Example: baseline 30 umms → target about 20 or fewer. Baseline 9 long pauses → target about 6 or fewer.
- **You can speak for 2 minutes on a random topic with zero preparation**, without stopping for more than 5 seconds.
- **Connectors: keep at least 5** different ones (your Month 2 level), and aim for **6**.

**Results table** (copy into your nightly log):

| | Baseline (Week 1 Day 1) | Month 1 check | Month 2 check | Month 3 check (tonight) | Month 3 target | Hit it? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Umms / uhhs | | | | | baseline × 0.67 | |
| Pauses over 2 seconds | | | | | baseline × 0.67 | |
| Different connectors | | | | | 5 or more (aim 6) | |
| Talked 2 min, random topic, no prep? | — | — | — | yes / no | yes | |

**Compare three recordings.** Play 30 seconds of your baseline, 30 seconds of your Month 2 check, and 30 seconds of tonight. Don't only count — listen. Write one sentence for each question:

1. Do I start talking faster after the question?
2. When I'm missing a word, do I go around it now instead of stopping?
3. Do I sound more relaxed? Is my speed closer to the people I shadow?

**If you hit the targets:** great — you're ready for Month 4, where the pressure moves from "no preparation" to "real people watching". Move on.

**If you missed a target, don't just push on.** Do this:

| Missed target | What to do in Week 13 (no extra time — swap it into your drill block) |
|---|---|
| Umms still high | Do 10 minutes of [random-topic drill](../../toolkit/random-topic-drill.md) at the start of every drill block, and replace each "umm" with a real phrase from [time-buyers](../../month-01-foundations/week-03-small-talk/day-4-time-buyers.md) or [sentence starters](../../month-02-speed/week-08-automatic-answers/day-3-sentence-starter-bank.md). |
| Long pauses still high | Redo [Week 11 Day 1: Paraphrasing](../week-11-never-stopping/day-1-paraphrasing.md) exercises once, and in voice mode ask Claude to say "Go!" whenever you're silent for 2 seconds. |
| Fewer than 5 connectors | Before each drill, pick 3 connectors from [Week 1 Day 1](../../month-01-foundations/week-01-storytelling/day-1-storytelling-connectors.md) and force them into every story. |
| Couldn't talk 2 minutes | Do 1-minute random topics until they feel easy, then 90 seconds, then 2 minutes. Use the Day 6 cards you didn't pick tonight. |

Send your results table to Claude with the report above and ask: *"Should I change anything in Month 4 based on these numbers?"*
