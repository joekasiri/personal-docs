# Month 3: Spontaneity
[Programme home](../README.md) · [← Month 2: Speed](../month-02-speed/README.md) · [Month 4: Real-life pressure →](../month-04-real-life-pressure/README.md)

## What this month is about

For two months you have practised with a safety net. In Month 1 you told stories you had time to plan. In Month 2 you told them faster, and you built a bank of ready-made answers and sentence starters. That was the right order: first build the pieces, then make them fast. But real conversation doesn't give you a topic the night before. Someone says "Wait, what's the weirdest thing you own?" and you have two seconds.

This month you **stop preparing**. From tonight, nearly every drill starts with a topic or a question you have not seen before, and you start talking within two seconds. No notes, no planning, no "let me just think for a minute". This is where "talking without thinking" really begins, because your brain learns that it *can* find words in real time, and that a slightly messy answer is far better than silence.

The month has four steps. In **Week 9** you talk on the spot about everyday things: objects, places, people, experiences and "would you rather" choices. In **Week 10** you handle hypotheticals: "If I could…", "If I hadn't…", "I wish…", "What if…?" and plans for the future, which come up all the time in casual chat and feel hard because the grammar is long. In **Week 11** you learn to *never stop*: going around missing words, correcting yourself smoothly, talking about things you don't really know, and finding your way back when you lose your train of thought. In **Week 12** you put it all together in role-plays: ordering, complaining, making plans, meeting new people, asking for favours and arguing a side you don't agree with.

Everything from Months 1 and 2 is still here. You will recycle your [connectors](../month-01-foundations/week-01-storytelling/day-1-storytelling-connectors.md), [answer, add, ask back](../month-01-foundations/week-03-small-talk/day-3-answer-add-ask.md), [opinions](../month-02-speed/week-06-opinions/day-1-giving-opinions.md) and your [sentence-starter bank](../month-02-speed/week-08-automatic-answers/day-3-sentence-starter-bank.md) in every drill. The difference is that now you have to grab them *without knowing what's coming*. That is exactly what happens in a real conversation.

The time split changes too: **less studying, more speaking**. The main skill block gets shorter (30 minutes) and the speaking drill gets longer (60 minutes). You also move from one live conversation a week to **two**.

## Goals for the month

By the end of Month 3 you can:

1. Pick a random topic and talk about it for **2 minutes with zero preparation**, starting within 2 seconds.
2. Answer "would you rather", "what would you do if" and "what if" questions immediately, using short spoken forms (I'd, woulda, shoulda, gonna).
3. Keep going when you're missing a word, by describing it ("it's like a…, the thing you use to…") instead of freezing.
4. Correct yourself mid-sentence smoothly ("I mean…", "or rather…") and get back on track after losing your thread.
5. Improvise in everyday role-plays (ordering, complaining, making plans, meeting someone, asking for a favour) without a script.
6. Have **two live conversations a week** and use at least three of the week's skills in each one.
7. Hit this month's numbers: umms and long pauses **about a third lower** than your Week 1 Day 1 baseline.

## Before you start

- Finish the [Month 2 check](../month-02-speed/week-08-automatic-answers/day-6-weekly-check.md) and write your numbers in the [nightly log](../toolkit/nightly-log.md). You'll compare with them at the end of this month.
- Read the [random-topic drill](../toolkit/random-topic-drill.md). It's the main drill of this month. Look through the [random topic cards](../toolkit/random-topics.md) once, but **don't** practise any of them in advance.
- Reread the [3/2/1 drill](../toolkit/drill-3-2-1.md). You still do one 3/2/1 round on Days 1 and 5.
- Check your [voice mode setup](../toolkit/voice-mode-with-claude.md). You use it on Days 2 and 4 every week.
- Plan your live conversations. You need **two a week** (Day 3 and Day 6). Look at [live conversations](../toolkit/live-conversations.md) and book a regular slot now: a language exchange partner, a weekly meetup, an online tutor, or a colleague you can grab a coffee with.
- Refresh the two lessons you'll lean on most: [time-buyers](../month-01-foundations/week-03-small-talk/day-4-time-buyers.md) and the [sentence-starter bank](../month-02-speed/week-08-automatic-answers/day-3-sentence-starter-bank.md). They are your two seconds of thinking time.
- Accept this now: your answers this month **will** be messy. That's the point. A messy answer you didn't stop is a win.

## How your nights change this month

| Block | Month 2 | Month 3 | What you do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shadowing | 20 min | **15 min** | Speak-along with whole clips, no pausing ([shadowing](../toolkit/shadowing.md)). Each week has its own sound or speaking feature. |
| Main skill | 40 min | **30 min** | One short lesson a night, used straight away in the drill. |
| Speaking drill | 45 min | **60 min** | See the table below. |
| Review | 15 min | 15 min | Spaced review **out loud**: yesterday's, last week's and last month's phrases ([phrase log](../toolkit/phrase-log.md)). |
| Live conversations | 1 a week | **2 a week** | Day 3 (30 min) and Day 6 (60 min). Voice mode with Claude is the backup. |

**The speaking drill, day by day:**

| Day | Speaking drill (60 min) |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | [Random-topic drill](../toolkit/random-topic-drill.md) with tonight's topic cards + one [3/2/1](../toolkit/drill-3-2-1.md) round |
| Day 2 | Voice mode with Claude ([how to](../toolkit/voice-mode-with-claude.md)) |
| Day 3 | 30 min drill + 30 min **live conversation #1** ([how to](../toolkit/live-conversations.md)) |
| Day 4 | Voice mode with Claude |
| Day 5 | Random-topic drill + one 3/2/1 round |
| Day 6 | Re-record test + **live conversation #2** |
| Day 7 | Light day: 30 minutes of listening, then rest |

Topic length: **1 minute** per topic in Weeks 9–10, **2 minutes** per topic in Weeks 11–12.

## The 4 weeks

| Week | Focus | You'll be able to… |
|---|---|---|
| [Week 9: Talking on the spot](week-09-talking-on-the-spot/README.md) | Objects, places, people, experiences, "would you rather" | talk for a minute about any everyday thing the moment it's named |
| [Week 10: Hypotheticals](week-10-hypotheticals/README.md) | If I could…, If I hadn't…, I wish…, What if…?, future plans | answer imaginary and future questions straight away, with natural spoken forms |
| [Week 11: Never stopping](week-11-never-stopping/README.md) | Paraphrasing, self-correction, being unsure, staying on track, summarising | keep talking when a word is missing or your mind goes blank |
| [Week 12: Role-plays](week-12-role-plays/README.md) | Ordering, complaining, plans, meeting people, favours, arguing the other side | improvise real-life situations without a script |

## End-of-month check

[Week 12 Day 6](week-12-role-plays/day-6-weekly-check.md) is your Month 3 check. You record a **2-minute talk on a random topic you haven't seen**, count your [3 numbers](../toolkit/the-3-numbers.md), and follow the [monthly check](../toolkit/monthly-check.md) procedure. You compare the result with your [Week 1 Day 1 baseline](../month-01-foundations/week-01-storytelling/day-1-storytelling-connectors.md) and your [Month 2 check](../month-02-speed/week-08-automatic-answers/day-6-weekly-check.md).

**Targets for Month 3:**

| Number | Target |
|---|---|
| Umms and uhhs | about **33% lower** than your baseline |
| Long pauses (over 2 seconds) | about **33% lower** than your baseline |
| Different connectors | keep at least **5** (your Month 2 level), aim for 6 |
| Random topic | you talk for the **full 2 minutes** with zero preparation |

If you miss a target, don't just push on into Month 4. The monthly check tells you what to repeat and what to tell Claude.
