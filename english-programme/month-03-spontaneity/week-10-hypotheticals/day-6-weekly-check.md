- Have ready: recording app, [the 3 numbers](../../toolkit/the-3-numbers.md) counting sheet, [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md), last week's numbers from [Week 9's weekly check](../week-09-talking-on-the-spot/day-6-weekly-check.md).
- The test prompts and checklists are in [Day 6 materials](exercises.md#day-6-materials).

## 1. Shadowing (free choice, 15 min)

Go back to the **hardest clip of the week**. For most people that's Day 2 (woulda / coulda) or Day 4 (fast "wuh-DIF" / "whuddya" questions). Speak along with the whole clip twice, no pausing. Then do it a third time and record yourself. Listen to 30 seconds: are your "woulda"s short and quiet, with the stress on the next word? See [shadowing](../../toolkit/shadowing.md).

## 2. Re-record test (15 min)

**No preparation.** Read the prompt, start the recorder, and start talking within 2 seconds. Talk for **2 minutes**.

> **Prompt:** "Think about the last few years of your life. Tell me about one thing that would be different now if you'd made a different choice, what you wish you'd done, and what you're gonna do next."

This prompt needs every pattern from this week: the mixed conditional (If I'd…, I'd be…), a wish or regret, and future forms. Try to use at least one of each, but don't stop to plan.

Then:

1. Listen back once.
2. Count your [3 numbers](../../toolkit/the-3-numbers.md): umms/uhhs, pauses over 2 seconds, different connectors.
3. Use the hypotheticals checklist in [Day 6 materials](exercises.md#day-6-materials) to tick which patterns you used.
4. Write the numbers in your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md), next to last week's.

## 3. Skill check (15 min)

Rate yourself honestly: **1** = I can't do it yet, **2** = I can do it, but slowly, **3** = it comes out without thinking. Say one example out loud for each before you rate it.

| Day | Topic | I can… | Rating (1–3) | If you scored 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | If I could… | answer "What would you do if…?" in under 2 seconds with "Honestly, I'd probably…" and keep going for a minute | | Redo [Exercise 1.3](exercises.md#day-1-exercises) with 5 new cards from [Day 1](day-1-if-i-could.md) |
| 2 | If I hadn't… | tell a sliding-doors story with "If I hadn't…, I would've…" and "I'd probably still be…" | | Redo [Exercise 2.2](exercises.md#day-2-exercises), then tell your story to Claude in voice mode |
| 3 | Wishes and regrets | share a light regret with "I really should've…" and use "I wish + past" correctly | | Redo [Exercise 3.1](exercises.md#day-3-exercises) and say every answer twice, faster |
| 4 | What-if questions | ask what-ifs 5 ways and answer a crazy one using play along → pick → push | | Redo [Exercise 4.2](exercises.md#day-4-exercises) and do 10 minutes of the game with Claude |
| 5 | Future plans and predictions | choose gonna / will / -ing / might / I'm thinking of fast, with "probably" in the right place | | Redo [Exercise 5.1](exercises.md#day-5-exercises) out loud with a 3-second limit |

Any topic you rate **1**: do the "If you scored 1" task on Day 7 (it can replace your listening) or add 10 minutes to Week 11 Day 1. Any topic you rate **2**: use it at least 3 times in tonight's conversation.

## 4. Live conversation (60 min)

This is **live conversation #2** of the week. Tips: [live conversations](../../toolkit/live-conversations.md).

**Your mission: run a "what would you do" conversation.** Bring 3 what-if questions and use them naturally when the chat slows down. Your goal is that the other person talks about their *imaginary* life and their *real* plans, and that you share yours too.

Things to try (tick them off afterwards with the mission checklist in [Day 6 materials](exercises.md#day-6-materials)):

1. Ask one fun what-if from your list. Answer it yourself with the 3P method: play along, pick, push.
2. Ask: "What's something you'd do if you had more time?" and answer it with "Honestly, I'd probably…".
3. Tell one 1-minute sliding-doors story: "If I hadn't…, I would've never…".
4. Share one light regret with "I really should've…" and finish with "Lesson learned."
5. Before you say goodbye, ask about their plans: "So what are you up to this week?" and share yours with "I'm thinking of…" or "I'll probably…".

**Backup plan:** if the live conversation falls through, do 45 minutes of voice mode with Claude, then 15 minutes of notes. Paste this:

```text
Let's do a 45-minute casual voice conversation in American English, like two new friends getting to know each other at a meetup. I'm a B2 learner. This week I practised hypotheticals: "If I could…, I'd…", "If I hadn't…, I would've…", "I wish I'd…", "What if…?" and future forms (gonna, I'll probably, I'm thinking of, might). Please:
- Talk about everyday things, but naturally bring in what-if questions, questions about past decisions, regrets, and future plans.
- Ask me one question at a time and keep your turns short.
- Ask me "What would you do if…?" at least 5 times and let me ask you some too.
- At the end, tell me which hypothetical patterns I used well, which I avoided, and my top 3 mistakes with corrections.
Start by asking what I'm up to this weekend.
```

1. Do a 5-minute spaced review out loud: this week's best 5 phrases, 5 from [Week 9](../week-09-talking-on-the-spot/README.md), 5 from [Month 2](../../month-02-speed/README.md). See [phrase log](../../toolkit/phrase-log.md).
2. Send Claude this message (fill in the gaps):

```text
Week 10 (Hypotheticals) weekly check results:
- Re-record (2 min, unprepared): umms/uhhs = __, pauses over 2 seconds = __, different connectors = __
- Last week (Week 9): umms/uhhs = __, pauses = __, connectors = __
- Hypotheticals I used in the re-record: __ (out of: second conditional, third/mixed conditional, wish/regret, what-if, future forms)
- Skill check ratings (1–3): If I could __, If I hadn't __, Wishes and regrets __, What-if questions __, Future plans __
- Live conversations this week: __ out of 2. What went well: __. Where I froze: __.
Please tell me (1) what to focus on next week, (2) 3 short drills for my weakest pattern, and (3) one thing I'm doing better than last week.
```

3. **Carry into next week:** next week is [Never stopping](../week-11-never-stopping/README.md). Write down the moments this week where you froze because you didn't know a word. Week 11 Day 1 ([Paraphrasing](../week-11-never-stopping/day-1-paraphrasing.md)) teaches you how to go around those words. Keep using "I'd probably…" and "I'm thinking of…" every day — they are now part of your automatic answers.
