# Week 9 exercises: Talking on the spot
[Week 9 overview](README.md) · [Day 1](day-1-talking-about-objects.md) · [Day 2](day-2-talking-about-places.md) · [Day 3](day-3-talking-about-people.md) · [Day 4](day-4-talking-about-experiences.md) · [Day 5](day-5-would-you-rather.md) · [Day 6](day-6-weekly-check.md)

**How to use these exercises:** do everything **out loud**. Never just read silently. When an exercise says "record", record it on your phone and listen back to at least one answer. When there's a time limit, use a timer and respect it: the point this month is speed, not perfection. If a sentence comes out wrong, don't restart. Keep going, and fix it next time. Model answers are examples, not the "right" answer: your own true answers are always better.

## Day 1 exercises

### Exercise 1.1: Object chunks, 5 times each

**Time:** 8 min · **Type:** out loud

1. Read the chunk and the example once.
2. Say the example out loud 5 times, faster each time. By the fifth time, don't look at the page.
3. Then make **one new sentence** with the chunk about something **you** own, and say it twice.

| # | Chunk | Example to repeat 5 times |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | It's one of those… that… | It's one of those little speakers that clips onto your bag. |
| 2 | It's basically a… | It's basically a notebook, but you can erase the pages. |
| 3 | I've had it for like… | I've had this jacket for like eight years. |
| 4 | It's got these… | It's got these tiny buttons that I always press by accident. |
| 5 | Someone gave it to me… | My sister gave it to me when I moved into my first apartment. |
| 6 | I use it to… | I use it to block out noise on the train. |
| 7 | The thing about it is… | The thing about it is, it's way too heavy. |
| 8 | It's seen better days | My laptop's seen better days. The screen's kinda cracked. |
| 9 | I could totally live without it | Honestly, I could totally live without my smartwatch. |
| 10 | Don't ask me why, but… | Don't ask me why, but I still keep the box. |

### Exercise 1.2: Upgrade the flat answer

**Time:** 8 min · **Type:** out loud, then compare

Each answer below is correct but flat. It would end the conversation.

1. Read the question and the flat answer.
2. Out loud, give a **better answer of 3–4 sentences** using the 4-step shape (what it is → my one → a moment → what I think) and at least one chunk from tonight. You can invent details.
3. Then read the model upgrade in the [Day 1 answer key](#day-1-answer-key) and say it once out loud.

1. "What's that on your desk?" — "It is a plant."
2. "Do you like your phone?" — "Yes. It is good."
3. "What's in your bag?" — "My laptop, my keys and my wallet."
4. "Nice jacket!" — "Thank you."
5. "Do you still use a paper notebook?" — "Yes, I do."
6. "What's one thing you couldn't live without?" — "My coffee machine."
7. "Is that a new watch?" — "No."
8. "What's the most useless thing you own?" — "A bread machine."

### Exercise 2.3: Five senses in 30 seconds

**Time:** 10 min · **Type:** record

A place becomes real when you describe what you **see, hear, smell, feel** and **who's there**. This exercise trains your brain to grab those details fast.

1. For each place, take **no thinking time**. Start the timer and talk for 30 seconds.
2. In each answer, say at least **one thing you see, one thing you hear, and one thing you smell or feel**, plus who's there.
3. Talk about a **specific** one you know ("the supermarket near my apartment"), not supermarkets in general.
4. Record them all in one go.

**Places:**

2. Your office or workplace on a busy morning
3. A train station or bus station
4. A gym
5. Your favourite restaurant
6. A beach you've been to
7. A hospital or doctor's waiting room
8. An airport at night
9. Your grandparents' home (or an older relative's home)
10. A park on a Sunday afternoon
11. A concert or a stadium
12. Your school when you were a kid
13. A hotel room you remember
14. A street market
15. A coffee shop where people work on laptops
16. A traffic jam

### Exercise 3.4: Three people, one minute each

3. Then do the **challenge round**: pick one of the three again and talk for 1 minute, but **without using the adjective you used the first time**.

**Group C (people around you):** a neighbour · your barber or hairdresser · someone at your gym · the owner of a shop you go to · a friend of a friend

→ "Yeah, I've been to Mexico twice. The first time, I went with my parents, back in 2015."
