# Week 9: Talking on the spot
[Programme home](../../README.md) › [Month 3: Spontaneity](../README.md) · [← Week 8: Automatic answers](../../month-02-speed/week-08-automatic-answers/README.md) · [Week 10: Hypotheticals →](../week-10-hypotheticals/README.md)

## This week's goal

This week you learn to talk **immediately** about whatever comes up, with no preparation. Each night has one everyday subject: objects, places, people, experiences, and "would you rather" choices. For each one you get a simple "shape" for your answer, a set of chunks that fit almost any topic in that group, and a pile of topic cards you have never seen. You pick a card, and you start talking within two seconds. By Day 6 you can talk for a full minute about anything ordinary: your phone, your local supermarket, your old teacher, the worst flight you ever took, or whether you'd rather lose your phone or your wallet.

## How this builds on last week

In [Week 8](../../month-02-speed/week-08-automatic-answers/README.md) you built automatic answers: [60-second answers](../../month-02-speed/week-08-automatic-answers/day-1-sixty-second-answers.md) about yourself, [rapid-fire short answers](../../month-02-speed/week-08-automatic-answers/day-2-rapid-fire-answers.md) and a [sentence-starter bank](../../month-02-speed/week-08-automatic-answers/day-3-sentence-starter-bank.md). Those answers were about topics you could predict. This week, the topics are **unpredictable**. You still use your starters to begin, but the rest of the answer is built in real time. Think of it as moving from "answers I've practised" to "answers I make up as I go".

## Day 7: light day

30 minutes only. Find a casual "day in my life" or "room tour" vlog by an American creator (search YouTube for "**day in my life vlog chatty**", "**apartment tour talking**" or "**what's in my bag vlog**") and watch it on your phone while you walk, or listen with the screen off. Your one job: notice **how they talk about ordinary things** without planning. Every time they describe an object or a place, listen for the chunks from this week ("it's one of those…", "the thing about it is…", "it's kind of a…"). At the end, say out loud one thing the person talked about and what *you* would say about your own version of it, for 30 seconds. Then rest. No recording, no logging.

## By the end of this week you can…

- [ ] Talk for 1 minute about any everyday object, starting within 2 seconds.
- [ ] Describe a place so the listener can picture it, using the "where, what's it like, how it feels, a moment there" shape.
- [ ] Describe a person you know: what they're like, a typical thing they do, and a short story about them.
- [ ] Answer "Have you ever…?" and "What's the best/worst… you've ever…?" with a quick, real story.
- [ ] Answer any "would you rather" question straight away, give a reason, and ask it back.
- [ ] Say "th" clearly in fast speech without slowing down.
- [ ] Have two live conversations this week and use at least three of these skills in each.
