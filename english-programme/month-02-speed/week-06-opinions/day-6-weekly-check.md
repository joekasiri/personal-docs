# Week 6 · Day 6: Weekly check
[Programme home](../../README.md) › [Month 2](../README.md) › [Week 6](README.md) · [← Previous: Hedging and "it depends"](day-5-hedging-and-it-depends.md) · [Next: Week 7 · Day 1: Explaining how things work →](../week-07-explaining/day-1-explaining-how-things-work.md)

**Tonight (2 hours):** Shadowing 20 min · Re-record test 20 min · Skill check 15 min · Live conversation 45 min · Review and report 20 min

## Before you start

- Finish Days [1](day-1-giving-opinions.md) to [5](day-5-hedging-and-it-depends.md).
- Open your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md) with your baseline, your Month 1 check, and last week's Day 6 numbers.
- Confirm tonight's live conversation. Backup plan in section 4.
- Have the [Day 6 materials](exercises.md#day-6-materials) open: the test prompt, the opinion checklist and the live-conversation question cards are there.

## 1. Shadowing (free choice, 20 min)

Go back to the **hardest clip of the week**. For most people that's the Day 3 disagreement clip or the Day 5 fall-rise clip.

1. Speak along 3 times without stopping. Record the third.
2. Compare with the speaker: is your stress on the same words? Does your voice go down and up on the hedges?
3. Last 5 minutes: speak along with your favourite clip of the week, just for fun, with exaggerated stress.

Method reminder: [Shadowing](../../toolkit/shadowing.md).

## 2. Re-record test (20 min)

**Tonight's unprepared prompt:**

> **"What's something most people love that you don't really like? Why?"**

Don't plan. Start your recording and start talking within 5 seconds.

1. Record **2 minutes**. Stop exactly at 2:00.
2. Count [the 3 numbers](../../toolkit/the-3-numbers.md): umms/uhhs, pauses over 2 seconds, different connectors. Opinion chunks ("If you ask me…"), reason chunks ("The main reason is…") and hedges ("I guess it depends…") count as connectors.
3. Listen again with the **opinion checklist** in [Day 6 materials](exercises.md#day-6-materials).
4. Write the 3 numbers in your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md) next to last week's.

## 3. Skill check (15 min)

Rate yourself: **1** = can't do it without looking, **2** = can do it slowly, **3** = comes out without thinking.

| Day | Topic | I can… | Rating (1–3) | If you scored 1… |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Giving opinions](day-1-giving-opinions.md) | start an opinion with a chunk in under 3 seconds and show how strong it is | | Redo Exercise 1.3 (3-second rule) with a timer |
| 2 | [Agreeing](day-2-agreeing.md) | agree in different ways and add my own point | | Redo Exercise 2.1, and agree out loud with 5 things you read online tomorrow |
| 3 | [Disagreeing politely](day-3-disagreeing-politely.md) | disagree with acknowledge → but → view → reason, and stay friendly | | Redo Exercise 3.2 and 3.3 |
| 4 | [Giving reasons and examples](day-4-giving-reasons-and-examples.md) | support an opinion with a reason and a personal example (O-R-E-O) | | Redo Exercise 4.3, recording every answer |
| 5 | [Hedging and "it depends"](day-5-hedging-and-it-depends.md) | hedge, say what it depends on, give both cases and my lean | | Redo Exercise 5.2 |

Any **1**: add 10 minutes to the main-skill block on Days 1 and 2 next week for the suggested exercise. Next week's explaining skills also need reasons and examples, so Day 4 skills will keep getting practice.

## 4. Live conversation (45 min)

Tonight's mission: **share opinions and react to someone else's.** See [Live conversations](../../toolkit/live-conversations.md).

**Try these 5 things:**

1. Ask at least 3 opinion questions from the **question cards** in [Day 6 materials](exercises.md#day-6-materials) (choose ones that fit the person).
2. Agree at least once **and add** something ("Totally. And…") (Day 2).
3. Disagree politely at least once, even on something small ("I see what you mean, but…") (Day 3).
4. Give at least one opinion with a real example ("Like, take my…") (Day 4).
5. Hedge once and give your lean ("I guess it depends… but if I had to choose…") (Day 5).

Choose light topics with people you don't know well (food, cities, films, apps, work habits). Save more serious topics for people you know.

**Backup plan:** if your partner cancels, do 30 minutes of voice mode ([Voice mode with Claude](../../toolkit/voice-mode-with-claude.md)) with this prompt:

```
Pretend you're a friendly American I just met at a meetup. Let's chat casually. Ask me for my opinion on everyday things (food, cities, work, apps, travel) and share your own opinions too. Sometimes agree with me, sometimes disagree politely, so I can practise both. Don't correct me during the chat. At the end, tell me the 3 things I did best and my 3 most important mistakes, with the natural way to say them.
```

## 5. Review and report (20 min)

1. Write in your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md): tonight's 3 numbers, your skill-check ratings, and one sentence about the live conversation.
2. Say your **10 best phrases of the week** from memory.
3. Send Claude this message (fill in the brackets):

```

Re-record test numbers: umms [ ], long pauses [ ], connectors [ ].
Last week: umms [ ], long pauses [ ], connectors [ ]. Baseline: umms [ ], long pauses [ ], connectors [ ].

```

**Carry into next week:** in Week 7 you'll explain decisions and compare things. That's opinions with more structure, so keep using O-R-E-O and your hedges.
