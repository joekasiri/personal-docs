# Week 7: Explaining
[Programme home](../../README.md) › [Month 2: Speed](../README.md) · [← Week 6](../week-06-opinions/README.md) · [Week 8 →](../week-08-automatic-answers/README.md)

## This week's goal

Explaining is everywhere: how to use an app, why you chose your apartment, which laptop is better, what went wrong with a project, how your coffee machine works. It's also where speakers get lost, because explanations have **many small steps** and it's easy to lose your place. This week you learn to explain in a clear order, in short "thought groups" that the listener can follow, and to check that they're still with you. You'll explain how things work, explain decisions, compare things, describe problems and solutions, and check understanding. By Day 6 you should be able to explain something from your life or work for 1–2 minutes without the listener getting lost and without you freezing in the middle.

## How this builds on last week

Last week was [Opinions](../week-06-opinions/README.md). An explanation of a decision or a comparison is really an opinion with more structure: you'll keep using reasons and examples from [Day 4: giving reasons and examples](../week-06-opinions/day-4-giving-reasons-and-examples.md), and hedges from [Day 5](../week-06-opinions/day-5-hedging-and-it-depends.md) when comparisons aren't simple. In Month 1 you talked about [your job at a basic level](../../month-01-foundations/week-04-vocabulary/day-5-talking-about-your-job.md). This week you'll explain things *from* your work (a tool, a decision, a problem) in a way anyone can follow. Deeper work talk comes in Month 5.

## Day 7: light day

30 minutes only. Walk and listen to a relaxed explainer podcast or video where one person explains something to another (search "explained podcast casual", "science chat podcast for beginners" or "how stuff works chat"). Your one task: **count the "checks"**, every time a speaker says something like "right?", "you know?", "does that make sense?" or "so basically…". Notice how often natives check. Note your favourite 3 in your phrase log at home. Then rest.

## By the end of this week you can…

- [ ] Explain a process in clear steps ("First you…, then…, once you've…").
- [ ] Explain why you (or your team) made a decision, including the options you didn't choose.
- [ ] Compare two things clearly and say which one is better for what.
- [ ] Describe a problem, what caused it and how it was fixed.
- [ ] Check that your listener understands, and rephrase when they don't.
- [ ] Speak in short thought groups with natural pauses instead of one long, breathless sentence.
- [ ] Explain something from your life or work for 1–2 minutes without losing your place.
