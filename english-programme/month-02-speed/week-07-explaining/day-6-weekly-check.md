# Week 7 · Day 6: Weekly check
[Programme home](../../README.md) › [Month 2](../README.md) › [Week 7](README.md) · [← Previous: Checking understanding](day-5-checking-understanding.md) · [Next: Week 8 · Day 1: Sixty-second answers →](../week-08-automatic-answers/day-1-sixty-second-answers.md)

**Tonight (2 hours):** Shadowing 20 min · Re-record test 20 min · Skill check 15 min · Live conversation 45 min · Review and report 20 min

## Before you start

- Finish Days [1](day-1-explaining-how-things-work.md) to [5](day-5-checking-understanding.md).
- Open your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md) with your baseline, Month 1 check and the last two Day 6 numbers.
- Confirm tonight's live conversation. Backup plan in section 4.
- Have the [Day 6 materials](exercises.md#day-6-materials) open: the test prompt, the explanation checklist and the "explain it to me" topics are there.

## 1. Shadowing (free choice, 20 min)

Go back to the **hardest clip of the week**. Usually that's the Day 1 thought-groups clip (lots of steps) or the Day 4 problem/solution clip.

1. Speak along 3 times without stopping. Record the third.
2. Compare: are your pauses between thought groups in the same places as the speaker's?
3. Last 5 minutes: choose any explainer clip you haven't used and speak along with the first minute "cold" (no listening first). It's hard. That's practice for Month 3.

Method reminder: [Shadowing](../../toolkit/shadowing.md).

## 2. Re-record test (20 min)

**Tonight's unprepared prompt:**

> **"Explain something you're good at, and how someone could learn it."**

No planning. Start recording and start talking within 5 seconds.

1. Record **2 minutes**. Stop exactly at 2:00.
2. Count [the 3 numbers](../../toolkit/the-3-numbers.md): umms/uhhs, pauses over 2 seconds, different connectors. Sequence chunks ("Once you've…"), decision chunks ("It came down to…"), comparison chunks ("whereas…") and checks ("Does that make sense?") count.
3. Listen again with the **explanation checklist** in [Day 6 materials](exercises.md#day-6-materials).
4. Write the numbers in your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md).

## 3. Skill check (15 min)

Rate yourself: **1** = can't do it without looking, **2** = can do it slowly, **3** = comes out without thinking.

| Day | Topic | I can… | Rating (1–3) | If you scored 1… |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Explaining how things work](day-1-explaining-how-things-work.md) | explain a process with big picture → steps → tip → done | | Redo Exercise 1.3 with 3 new processes |
| 2 | [Explaining decisions](day-2-explaining-decisions.md) | explain a decision, the options and what it came down to | | Redo Exercise 2.1 |
| 3 | [Comparing things](day-3-comparing-things.md) | compare two things with modifiers, say what each is better for, and recommend | | Redo Exercise 3.2 |
| 4 | [Explaining problems and solutions](day-4-explaining-problems-and-solutions.md) | explain problem → impact → cause → fix → now | | Redo Exercise 4.3 |
| 5 | [Checking understanding](day-5-checking-understanding.md) | check naturally, pause, and rephrase when someone's lost | | Redo Exercise 5.3, then explain something to a friend or family member this week |

Any **1**: add 10 minutes to the main-skill block on Days 1 and 2 next week. In Week 8 you'll build 60-second answers, which use explaining skills ("What do you do?"), so they'll keep getting practice.

## 4. Live conversation (45 min)

Tonight's mission: **explain something to a real person, and check they understand.** See [Live conversations](../../toolkit/live-conversations.md).

**Try these 5 things:**

1. When they ask "What do you do?", explain your job in simple words with a big picture first (Day 1) and an everyday comparison.
2. Explain one decision you made recently (Day 2): a trip, an apartment, a course, a purchase.
3. Ask them to compare two things they know ("Which is better, X or Y?") and then compare two things yourself (Day 3).
4. Tell them about a problem you had and how you solved it (Day 4).
5. Check understanding at least twice, and confirm at least once when *they* explain something ("Got it. So you mean…?") (Day 5).

The "explain it to me" topics in [Day 6 materials](exercises.md#day-6-materials) are good questions to ask *them*, so you can practise listening and confirming too.

**Backup plan:** if your partner cancels, do 30 minutes of voice mode ([Voice mode with Claude](../../toolkit/voice-mode-with-claude.md)):

```
Pretend you're a friendly American I just met at a coworking space. Let's chat casually. Ask me what I do, why I chose it, and about a problem I've dealt with recently. Sometimes say "Wait, I don't get it" so I have to rephrase. Also explain one thing about your own "job" so I can practise confirming I understood. Don't correct me during the chat. At the end, tell me the 3 things I did best and my 3 most important mistakes, with the natural way to say them.
```

2. Say your **10 best phrases of the week** from memory.

**Carry into next week:** Week 8 is the speed review and your **Month 2 check**. Next week you'll build ready-made answers, so start noticing the questions people ask you most often.
