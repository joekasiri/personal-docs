# Week 5 · Day 6: Weekly check
[Programme home](../../README.md) › [Month 2](../README.md) › [Week 5](README.md) · [← Previous: Retelling films and shows](day-5-retelling-films-and-shows.md) · [Next: Week 6 · Day 1: Giving opinions →](../week-06-opinions/day-1-giving-opinions.md)

**Tonight (2 hours):** Shadowing 20 min · Re-record test 20 min · Skill check 15 min · Live conversation 45 min · Review and report 20 min

## Before you start

- Finish Days [1](day-1-setting-the-scene.md) to [5](day-5-retelling-films-and-shows.md).
- Have your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md) open with your baseline numbers, your Month 1 check numbers and this week's round 3 numbers.
- Confirm your live conversation for tonight (a meetup, a language exchange partner, a colleague, a tutor). If it falls through, use the backup plan in section 4.
- Have the [Day 6 materials](exercises.md#day-6-materials) open: the test prompt and the story checklist are there.

## 1. Shadowing (free choice, 20 min)

Go back to the **hardest clip of the week**. For most people that's the Day 2 suspense clip or the Day 3 clip with lots of voices.

1. Speak along with it 3 times without stopping.
2. Record the third time.
3. Compare with the speaker: are your pauses in the same places? Is your voice changing for quotes?
4. Last 5 minutes: speak along with your favourite 2-minute clip from Day 5, just for fun.

See [Shadowing](../../toolkit/shadowing.md) if you need a reminder of the method.

## 2. Re-record test (20 min)

**Tonight's unprepared prompt:**

> **"Tell me about the most surprising thing that's happened to you in the last year."**

Don't plan. Don't write anything. Read the prompt, start your recording, and start talking within 5 seconds.

1. Record **2 minutes**. Use a timer and stop exactly at 2:00.
2. Listen once and count [the 3 numbers](../../toolkit/the-3-numbers.md):
   - "umm"s and "uhh"s
   - pauses longer than 2 seconds
   - different connectors (not just "and" and "so"; this week's phrases like "Guess what?", "Needless to say…" and "I was like…" count)
3. Listen a second time with the **story checklist** from [Day 6 materials](exercises.md#day-6-materials) and tick what you did.
4. Write the 3 numbers in your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md) next to last week's.

## 3. Skill check (15 min)

Rate yourself honestly for each day: **1** = I can't do this without looking, **2** = I can do it slowly, **3** = it comes out without thinking.

| Day | Topic | I can… | Rating (1–3) | If you scored 1… |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Setting the scene](day-1-setting-the-scene.md) | open a story in under 10 seconds with when, where and who | | Redo Exercise 1.2 and say 5 openings before bed tomorrow |
| 2 | [Building suspense](day-2-building-suspense.md) | use a suspense phrase and a silent pause before the surprise | | Redo Exercise 2.3 and the Day 2 shadowing sentences |
| 3 | [Casual reported speech](day-3-casual-reported-speech.md) | report a conversation with *be like, go, be all, say* and change my voice | | Redo Exercise 3.2, then tell one quote-heavy story to yourself in the shower |
| 4 | [Endings and punchlines](day-4-endings-and-punchlines.md) | end a story with one clear line and stop | | Redo Exercise 4.1, and end every story this week with "So yeah." ↘ |
| 5 | [Retelling films and shows](day-5-retelling-films-and-shows.md) | retell a plot in about a minute, in the present tense, without spoilers | | Redo Exercise 5.2 with one invented show and one real one |

Any topic with a **1**: add 10 minutes to the main-skill block on Day 1 and Day 2 next week and redo the suggested exercise. Your Week 6 drills will also ask you to tell short stories, so you will keep practising.

## 4. Live conversation (45 min)

Tonight's mission: **tell one real story to a real person**, using this week's skills. See [Live conversations](../../toolkit/live-conversations.md) for how to find someone and how to deal with nerves.

**Try these 5 things:**

1. Ask them, "Watched anything good lately?" and, when they ask you back, retell a show in about a minute (Day 5).
2. Tell one personal story with a fast scene (Day 1): "So this was a couple weeks ago…"
3. Use at least one suspense moment with a real pause (Day 2).
4. Report at least two lines of dialogue with "I was like…" / "she goes…" (Day 3).
5. Finish with a clear ending and **stop** (Day 4). Notice how they react.

Also listen to *their* stories and react with [Week 1 reaction phrases](../../month-01-foundations/week-01-storytelling/day-2-reaction-phrases.md) and [follow-up questions](../../month-01-foundations/week-02-questions/day-2-follow-up-questions.md).

**Backup plan:** if your live partner cancels, do 30 minutes of voice mode with Claude ([Voice mode with Claude](../../toolkit/voice-mode-with-claude.md)) with this prompt, and still try to book a real conversation for next week:

```
Pretend you're a friendly American coworker I'm having coffee with. Ask me what I've been watching and what's new with me. When I tell a story, react naturally and ask follow-up questions. Tell me one or two stories of your own too. Don't correct me during the chat. At the end, tell me the 3 things I did best and the 3 most important mistakes, with the natural way to say them.
```

## 5. Review and report (20 min)

1. Write in your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md): tonight's 3 numbers, your skill-check ratings, and one sentence about the live conversation (how it felt, what worked).
2. Say your **10 best phrases of the week** from memory.
3. Send Claude this message (fill in the brackets):

```
Week 5 (Better stories) report.
Re-record test numbers: umms [ ], long pauses [ ], connectors [ ].
Last week's numbers: umms [ ], long pauses [ ], connectors [ ]. Baseline: umms [ ], long pauses [ ], connectors [ ].
Skill check (1-3): scene [ ], suspense [ ], reported speech [ ], endings [ ], retelling shows [ ].
Live conversation: [who / where, how long, how it felt].
What was hardest this week: [ ].
Please tell me: are my numbers going in the right direction for Month 2 (target: about 25% fewer umms and pauses than baseline, 5+ connectors)? What's one thing to focus on in Week 6 (Opinions)?
```

**Carry into next week:** keep telling short stories. In Week 6 you'll use a story as the *example* behind your opinions, so your scene-setting and endings will keep getting practice.
