# Week 5: Better stories
[Programme home](../../README.md) › [Month 2: Speed](../README.md) · [← Week 4](../../month-01-foundations/week-04-vocabulary/README.md) · [Week 6 →](../week-06-opinions/README.md)

## This week's goal

In Month 1 you learned to tell a story that is *correct*: it has connectors, a clear order and the right past tenses. This week you make your stories *good to listen to*, and you tell them faster. You will learn to open a story in one or two quick sentences, build a little suspense so people lean in, act out what people said with "I was like…" and "she goes…", and finish with an ending that tells the listener "OK, now you can laugh or react". On Day 5 you will retell a film or a series episode, which is one of the most common things people talk about. Every night ends with the new **3/2/1 drill**, so you practise telling the same story faster and faster.

## How this builds on last week

Last week you built everyday vocabulary: [phrasal verbs](../../month-01-foundations/week-04-vocabulary/day-1-phrasal-verbs.md), [collocations](../../month-01-foundations/week-04-vocabulary/day-2-collocations.md), [intensifiers and softeners](../../month-01-foundations/week-04-vocabulary/day-3-intensifiers-and-softeners.md) and [idioms](../../month-01-foundations/week-04-vocabulary/day-4-everyday-idioms.md). This week you put that vocabulary *inside stories*. You already know the [12 storytelling connectors](../../month-01-foundations/week-01-storytelling/day-1-storytelling-connectors.md) and the [setting → problem → turning point → ending structure](../../month-01-foundations/week-01-storytelling/day-3-story-structure.md). We won't re-teach them. Instead, each day takes one part of that structure and makes it faster and more lively.

## Day 7: light day

30 minutes only. Go for a walk and listen to one episode (or 30 minutes) of a casual two-person podcast where the hosts tell each other stories. Search for "friends podcast storytime" or "comedy podcast story episode". Your one task: **count how many different ways the speakers start a story** ("So the other day…", "OK, so you know how…", "This one time…"). Note the three you like best in your phrase log when you get home. Then rest. No recording today.

## By the end of this week you can…

- [ ] Open a story in one or two sentences that give the time, place and people.
- [ ] Use at least three suspense phrases ("And then…", "Guess what?", "You're not gonna believe this") with a real pause.
- [ ] Report what people said casually with "I was like…", "she goes…", "he was all…", using present tense when it feels natural.
- [ ] End a story clearly so your listener knows it's their turn to react.
- [ ] Retell the plot of a film or show in about a minute without spoilers.
- [ ] Tell a story in 1 minute that took you 3 minutes on the first try, without losing the best parts.
- [ ] Speak along with a storyteller clip for at least 30 seconds.
