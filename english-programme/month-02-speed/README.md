# Month 2: Speed
[Programme home](../README.md) · [← Month 1: Foundations](../month-01-foundations/README.md) · [Month 3: Spontaneity →](../month-03-spontaneity/README.md)

## What this month is about

In Month 1 you built the foundations: connectors for stories, questions, small talk, time-buyers and everyday vocabulary. You practised them slowly and carefully, and you rehearsed your stories with the 4/3/2 drill. This month you use the same kind of material, but **faster**. The whole month is about closing the gap between "I know how to say it" and "it just comes out".

Speed matters because real conversation does not wait for you. In a group, if you need five seconds to build a sentence, someone else is already talking. People who "talk without thinking" are not thinking faster than you. They are pulling out ready-made chunks that they have said hundreds of times. This month you will say your chunks so often, and under so much time pressure, that they start to feel like your own.

You will also add two new kinds of talk that come up in every casual conversation: **opinions** (what you think, agreeing, disagreeing, hedging) and **explaining** (how something works, why you decided something, comparing, problems and fixes). These are harder than stories because you cannot rehearse them as easily, so they are the perfect bridge to Month 3, where preparation stops completely.

Three things change in your nights. The speaking drill becomes **3/2/1** (shorter rounds, more pressure). Shadowing moves to faster speakers and adds **speak-along**, where you talk at the same time as the speaker without pausing. And in your review block you stop reading your phrases: you **say them from memory**, out loud.

The last week, "Automatic answers", is a speed review. You will build 60-second answers to the questions people always ask, practise rapid-fire short answers, collect sentence starters, and use contractions naturally in your own speech. By Day 6 of Week 8 you should tell stories and give opinions at close to normal speed.

## Goals for the month

By the end of Month 2 you can:

- Tell a 1-minute story with a clear scene, some suspense, at least two lines of casual reported speech ("I was like…", "she goes…") and a clean ending.
- Give an opinion with a reason and an example in under 30 seconds, and agree, disagree politely or hedge without freezing.
- Explain how something works, why a decision was made, or how two things compare, in a clear order that a listener can follow.
- Answer the 8 most common "getting to know you" questions in about 60 seconds each, without preparing on the day.
- Answer 30 rapid-fire questions in under 10 seconds each, with natural short answers.
- Speak along with a fast casual speaker for 1 minute without losing your place.
- Cut your "umm"s and long pauses by about **25%** compared with your Week 1 Day 1 baseline, and use at least **5 different connectors** in 2 minutes.

## Before you start

- Finish Month 1, including the month check: [Week 4 · Day 6: Weekly check](../month-01-foundations/week-04-vocabulary/day-6-weekly-check.md). Have your baseline numbers and your Month 1 numbers ready.
- Read the new drill: [The 3/2/1 drill](../toolkit/drill-3-2-1.md).
- Read the speak-along section of [Shadowing](../toolkit/shadowing.md). You will use it every night.

- Reread [Voice mode with Claude](../toolkit/voice-mode-with-claude.md). Every Day 4 is a voice session.
- Book your four live conversations now (one per week, on Day 6). See [Live conversations](../toolkit/live-conversations.md) for where to find partners and a backup plan.
- Keep using the [all-day habits](../toolkit/all-day-habits.md): describe what you are doing in English, in your head or out loud.

## How your nights change this month

| Block | Time | What you do in Month 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Shadowing | 20 min | 5 min pause-and-repeat with the lesson's practice sentences, then 15 min **speak-along** with a fast casual clip |
| Main skill | 40 min | One new skill per night (Days 1–5), practised out loud with the week's exercises |
| Speaking drill | 45 min | **3/2/1 drill** on Days 1, 2, 3 and 5 with specific prompts. **Voice mode with Claude** on Day 4 |
| Review | 15 min | Say your logged phrases **out loud from memory**: tonight's, then yesterday's, then a few from Month 1 |

- **Live conversations:** 1 per week, on Day 6.
- **Day 6:** re-record a 2-minute unprepared talk, count [the 3 numbers](../toolkit/the-3-numbers.md), check your skills, and have your live conversation.
- **Day 7:** light day, 30 minutes of listening only (each week's README gives you a task). The full weekly rhythm is in [Daily routine](../toolkit/daily-routine.md).

## End-of-month check

Week 8 Day 6 is your Month 2 check: [Week 8 · Day 6: Weekly check](week-08-automatic-answers/day-6-weekly-check.md). You will re-record a 2-minute unprepared talk, count your 3 numbers, and compare them with your Week 1 Day 1 baseline and your Month 1 check, following [the monthly check procedure](../toolkit/monthly-check.md).

**Month 2 targets (compared with your baseline):**

| Number | Target |
|---|---|
| "Umm"s and "uhh"s | down about 25% |
| Pauses longer than 2 seconds | down about 25% |
| Different connectors in 2 minutes | at least 5 |

Example: if your baseline had 20 umms and 8 long pauses, your target is about 15 umms and 6 long pauses. If you are far from the target, do not just push on: tell Claude your numbers and ask for a plan adjustment before you start [Month 3: Spontaneity](../month-03-spontaneity/README.md).
