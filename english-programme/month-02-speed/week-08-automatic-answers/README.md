# Week 8: Automatic answers
[Programme home](../../README.md) › [Month 2: Speed](../README.md) · [← Week 7](../week-07-explaining/README.md) · [Week 9 →](../../month-03-spontaneity/week-09-talking-on-the-spot/README.md)

## This week's goal

Some questions come up again and again: "Tell me about yourself", "What do you do?", "Where are you from?", "What do you do for fun?". Many quick questions too: "You coming tonight?", "Busy week?", "Do you like it here?". This week you make your answers to these questions **automatic**, so they come out in a second, at normal speed, without any searching. You'll build 60-second answers to the 8 most common "getting to know you" questions, practise rapid-fire short answers, collect a bank of sentence starters that get you talking immediately, make contractions a natural part of your own speech, and finally combine a story, an opinion and a question in one smooth turn. It's also your **Month 2 check**: on Day 6 you'll compare your numbers with your baseline.

## How this builds on last week

Weeks 5–7 taught you to tell [better stories](../week-05-better-stories/README.md), give [opinions](../week-06-opinions/README.md) and [explain things](../week-07-explaining/README.md). This week is a **speed review**: you take everything from Month 2 (and Month 1) and make it fast. "What do you do?" uses your [explaining](../week-07-explaining/day-1-explaining-how-things-work.md) skills and your [Month 1 job vocabulary](../../month-01-foundations/week-04-vocabulary/day-5-talking-about-your-job.md). Rapid-fire answers use [answer, add, ask back](../../month-01-foundations/week-03-small-talk/day-3-answer-add-ask.md) and [hedges](../week-06-opinions/day-5-hedging-and-it-depends.md). Day 5 puts a story, an opinion and a question together in one turn.

## Before you start

- Reread your Week 7 [Day 6 report](../week-07-explaining/day-6-weekly-check.md) and Claude's advice for your Month 2 check.
- Have your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md) open with your baseline and Month 1 check numbers. You'll need them on Day 6.
- Read [The monthly check](../../toolkit/monthly-check.md) once, so you know what happens on Day 6.
- Find 2–3 fast, very casual clips with lots of reduced forms ("lemme", "gotta", "dunno"): search YouTube for "day in my life vlog talking fast", "friends chatting podcast unfiltered", "street interview quick questions" or "Q and A vlog rapid fire".
- Write down your answers to "Where are you from?" and "What do you do?" as you say them **now**. You'll compare them with your Day 1 version.
- Book your Day 6 live conversation: [Live conversations](../../toolkit/live-conversations.md).

## Day 7: light day

30 minutes only. You've finished Month 2. Walk and listen to a very casual podcast or vlog where people chat fast (search "unfiltered friends podcast" or "chatty vlog walk and talk"). Your one task: **just enjoy it**, and notice how much more you catch now than in Week 1, especially the fast forms ("lemme", "gotta", "dunno", "would've"). Then rest. Next week, [Month 3: Spontaneity](../../month-03-spontaneity/README.md) starts, and preparation stops.

## By the end of this week you can…

- [ ] Answer the 8 most common "getting to know you" questions in about 60 seconds each, without preparing on the day.
- [ ] Answer 30 rapid-fire questions in under 10 seconds each, with natural short answers.
- [ ] Start any answer immediately with a sentence starter.
- [ ] Use contractions (I'd, I'll, it's been, should've…) in your own speech without thinking.
- [ ] Combine a short story, an opinion and a question in one turn.
- [ ] Understand and use the fast forms lemme, gimme, gotta, dunno, betcha and gotcha.
- [ ] See your Month 2 progress against your baseline in numbers.
