# Week 8 · Day 6: Weekly check

**Tonight (2 hours):** Shadowing 15 min · Re-record test 20 min · Skill check 10 min · Live conversation 45 min · Review and report 10 min · Monthly check 20 min

This is your **last night of Month 2** and your **Month 2 check**. Section 6 compares your numbers with your Week 1 Day 1 baseline and your Month 1 check.

## Before you start

- Finish Days [1](day-1-sixty-second-answers.md) to [5](day-5-combining-story-opinion-question.md).
- Open your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md). You need: your **baseline** numbers (Week 1, Day 1), your **Month 1 check** numbers, and the Day 6 numbers from Weeks 5, 6 and 7.
- Read [The monthly check](../../toolkit/monthly-check.md) if you haven't yet.
- Confirm tonight's live conversation. Backup plan in section 4.
- Have the [Day 6 materials](exercises.md#day-6-materials) open: the test prompt, the Month 2 checklist and the results table are there.

## 1. Shadowing (free choice, 15 min)

Choose the **hardest clip of the whole month** (Weeks 5–8). For many people it's the Week 5 suspense clip or this week's fast "unfiltered" clip.

1. Speak along twice without stopping. Record the second time.
2. Compare it with your first attempt at that clip if you recorded it. Notice how much better you keep up.

Method reminder: [Shadowing](../../toolkit/shadowing.md).

## 2. Re-record test (20 min)

**Tonight's unprepared prompt** (a story, like your baseline):

> **"Tell me about a time something didn't go the way you expected."**

No planning. Start recording and start talking within 5 seconds.

1. Record **2 minutes**. Stop exactly at 2:00.
2. Count [the 3 numbers](../../toolkit/the-3-numbers.md): umms/uhhs, pauses over 2 seconds, different connectors. Everything from Months 1 and 2 counts as a connector: story connectors, suspense phrases, quote verbs, endings, opinion chunks, bridges, sequence chunks.
3. Listen again with the **Month 2 checklist** in [Day 6 materials](exercises.md#day-6-materials).
4. Write the numbers in your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md). You'll use them in section 6.

## 3. Skill check (10 min)

Rate yourself: **1** = can't do it without looking, **2** = can do it slowly, **3** = comes out without thinking.

| Day | Topic | I can… | Rating (1–3) | If you scored 1… |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Sixty-second answers](day-1-sixty-second-answers.md) | answer the 8 "getting to know you" questions in about 60 seconds, without notes | | Redo Exercise 1.2 for the questions you found hardest, every day next week |
| 2 | [Rapid-fire answers](day-2-rapid-fire-answers.md) | answer quick questions in under 10 seconds with a short answer + add-on | | Redo the 30 rapid-fire questions from the Day 2 drill |
| 3 | [Sentence starter bank](day-3-sentence-starter-bank.md) | start any answer within 1 second with a starter | | Redo Exercise 3.2 |
| 4 | [Contractions in your own speech](day-4-contractions-in-your-own-speech.md) | use contractions as my default, including *it's been* and *should've* | | Redo Exercise 4.3 and listen for full forms in every recording next week |
| 5 | [Combining story, opinion and question](day-5-combining-story-opinion-question.md) | combine a story, an opinion and a question in one turn | | Redo Exercise 5.3 |

Next week, [Month 3](../../month-03-spontaneity/README.md) removes preparation completely, and all of these skills will be tested every night. Any **1**: spend 10 extra minutes on the suggested exercise on Days 1 and 2 of Week 9.

## 4. Live conversation (45 min)

Tonight's mission: **answer everything automatically.** Talk with someone new if you can (a meetup, a language exchange, a new colleague), because they'll ask you all the "getting to know you" questions. See [Live conversations](../../toolkit/live-conversations.md).

**Try these 5 things:**

1. Answer "Tell me about yourself" or "What do you do?" with your 60-second skeleton, at the right length for the moment (Day 1).
2. Answer at least 5 quick questions with a short answer + add-on, under 10 seconds (Day 2).
3. Start every answer within 1 second with a starter (Day 3). Count, in your head, how many times you froze.
4. Use contractions everywhere. Notice if you slip into "I am" or "I have" (Day 4).
5. At least once, do a full S-O-Q: mini story → opinion → question (Day 5).

Also use the best of Month 2: tell one lively story (Week 5), give and react to opinions (Week 6), explain one thing and check they understood (Week 7).

**Backup plan:** if your partner cancels, do 30 minutes of voice mode ([Voice mode with Claude](../../toolkit/voice-mode-with-claude.md)):

```
Pretend you're a friendly American I just met at a party, and you're really curious about me. Ask me all the usual "getting to know you" questions, plus lots of quick questions, plus a few opinion questions. Tell me short stories of your own too, and sometimes end them with a question for me. Go at normal native speed. Don't correct me during the chat. At the end, tell me the 3 things I did best, my 3 most important mistakes with the natural way to say them, and how natural and fast I sounded from 1 to 10.
```

## 5. Review and report (10 min)

1. Write in your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md): tonight's 3 numbers, your skill-check ratings and one sentence about the live conversation.
2. Say your **10 best phrases of the month** from memory.
3. The report to Claude is part of the monthly check: see the message at the end of section 6.

## 6. Monthly check (20 min)

Follow [The monthly check](../../toolkit/monthly-check.md). Tonight's re-record test (section 2) is your Month 2 recording.

**Step 1: fill in the results table (5 min).** Copy it from [Day 6 materials](exercises.md#day-6-materials) into your nightly log.

| | Baseline (Week 1 Day 1) | Month 1 check (Week 4) | Month 2 check (tonight) | Change vs baseline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Umms / uhhs | | | | % |
| Pauses > 2 seconds | | | | % |
| Different connectors | | | | |

**How to calculate the change:** (baseline − tonight) ÷ baseline × 100. Example: baseline 20 umms, tonight 14 → (20 − 14) ÷ 20 × 100 = **30% fewer**.

**Step 2: compare with the Month 2 targets (5 min).**

| Number | Month 2 target |
|---|---|
| Umms / uhhs | about **25% fewer** than baseline |
| Pauses > 2 seconds | about **25% fewer** than baseline |
| Different connectors in 2 minutes | at least **5** |

**Step 3: listen side by side (5 min).** Play 30 seconds of your baseline, then 30 seconds of tonight's recording. Write 3 differences you can *hear* (speed, energy, pauses, connectors, contractions, how you start).

**Step 4: decide (5 min).**

- **Targets met or close:** great. Move on to [Month 3: Spontaneity](../../month-03-spontaneity/README.md).
- **One number far from the target** (for example, pauses are only 10% down): tell Claude which one, and ask for a 10-minute daily fix to add to Month 3.
- **Numbers not going down at all, or going up:** don't just push on. Send the report below and ask Claude to adjust the plan before you start Week 9. Common reasons: preparing too much before recordings, recording when very tired, or skipping the review block.
