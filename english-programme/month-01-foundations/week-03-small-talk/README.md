# Week 3: Small talk
[Programme home](../../README.md) › [Month 1: Foundations](../README.md) · [← Week 2](../week-02-questions/README.md) · [Week 4 →](../week-04-vocabulary/README.md)

## This week's goal

Small talk is where most people freeze. It's fast, it's unpredictable, and there's no time to plan. But small talk is actually very **repetitive**: the same openers, the same kinds of answers, the same ways to keep it going and end it. That's great news, because repetitive things can be learned as chunks. This week you learn the full small-talk loop: **open** a chat ("How's it going?") and answer with more than "fine", **show interest** with echo questions ("You went to Japan?") and back-channels ("Uh-huh", "Oh yeah?"), keep it going with **answer, add, ask back**, replace "umm" with natural **time-buyers**, and finally **change the topic and end the chat** politely. All week, shadowing trains **weak forms** — the tiny squashed words (ta, fer, 'n, a, c'n, 'em) that make American speech sound so fast.

## How this builds on last week

In [Week 2](../week-02-questions/README.md) you made questions automatic: [direct](../week-02-questions/day-1-direct-questions.md), [follow-ups](../week-02-questions/day-2-follow-up-questions.md), [indirect](../week-02-questions/day-3-indirect-questions.md), [tags and short answers](../week-02-questions/day-4-question-tags-and-short-answers.md). This week those questions become the engine of a real chat. You'll also reuse your [Week 1 reactions](../week-01-storytelling/day-2-reaction-phrases.md) — echo questions build on them. And from this week, **every 4/3/2 story ends with you asking the listener a question**.

## Day 7: light day

**30 minutes only.** Go for a walk and listen to a **chatty podcast with two or three friends** who catch up about their week (search "two friends podcast chat", "best friends catch up podcast", "comedy podcast catching up").

Your listening task:
1. Listen to how the episode **opens**. What do they say in the first 30 seconds? Say it after them.
2. Count the **back-channels** the listener uses while the other person talks ("mm-hm", "right", "oh yeah?", "no way").
3. Catch **one topic change** ("Speaking of…", "Oh, that reminds me…") and **one ending** ("Alright, that's it for today"). Log them tomorrow.

Then stop and rest.

## By the end of this week you can…

- [ ] open a chat with a friend, a colleague or a stranger, and answer "How's it going?" with more than "fine"
- [ ] show interest with echo questions ("You did?", "Japan?") and back-channels ("Uh-huh", "Oh yeah?")
- [ ] answer, add one detail and ask back — every time, without thinking
- [ ] use time-buyers like "Let me think…" or "Good question" instead of "umm"
- [ ] change the topic smoothly and end a chat politely
- [ ] hear and say weak forms: "ta", "fer", "'n", "a", "c'n", "'em"
