## 4. Live conversation

Read [Live conversations](../../toolkit/live-conversations.md) first (5 minutes).

**Your mission:** have a 15–30 minute conversation and run **one full small-talk loop**: open, keep it going, change the topic, and end it yourself.

**Try to:**
1. **You** open the conversation, with an opener that fits the situation ("So how do you know…?", "How's your week going?").
2. Use **answer, add, ask back** at least **5 times**. Never give a one-word answer.
3. Use at least **2 echo questions** ("You did?", "Six hours?") and back-channel while they talk.
4. When a question is hard, use a **time-buyer** instead of "umm".
5. Change the topic **once** with "Speaking of…" or "That reminds me…", and **you end the chat** with a 3-step ending.

**A first line, ready to use:** *"Hey! How's your week going so far?"* — and your answer ready for when they ask back.

**Backup plan:** a 30-minute [voice mode session with Claude](../../toolkit/voice-mode-with-claude.md). Use the standard setup prompt and add: *"You're someone I've just met at a friend's barbecue. Make normal small talk with me. Give short answers sometimes so I have to work to keep it going. At some point, let the conversation go quiet so I have to change the topic. At the end, I'll try to end the chat politely."*

Afterwards, write in your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md): who, how long, how many answer-add-asks you did, which bridge and ending you used, one moment you got stuck.
