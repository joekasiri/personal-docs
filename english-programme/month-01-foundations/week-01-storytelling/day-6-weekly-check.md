**Tonight (2 hours):** Shadowing 15 min · Re-record test 20 min · Skill check 15 min · Live conversation 50 min · Review and report 20 min

## Before you start

- Have ready: phone for recording, timer, paper and pen for counting, your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md) with your baseline numbers.
- Confirm your live conversation: time, place or app, and who. If it fell through, use the backup plan in section 4.
- Read [The 3 numbers](../../toolkit/the-3-numbers.md) again if you're not sure how to count.
- Don't look at this week's lessons before the re-record. The test should be **unprepared**.

## 1. Shadowing (free choice, 15 min)

Go back to the **hardest clip of the week** — the one where you couldn't keep up. Do 15 minutes of pause-and-repeat ([Shadowing](../../toolkit/shadowing.md)). If you can't decide, use tonight's warm-up:

1. Day 1 — flap T: "I GOT a LOT of WATer." → "I godda lodda WADder."
2. Day 2 — reductions: "I'm gonna GRAB a COFfee. You wanna COME?"
3. Day 3 — linking: "Can you pick it UP and check it OUT?" → "pi-ki-DUP… che-ki-DOUT"
4. Day 4 — rhythm: "I was WALKing HOME when it started to RAIN."
5. Day 5 — all together: "It was kinda SKETCHy, but the VIEW was AMAZing."

## 2. Re-record test (20 min)

1. Start recording. Name the file `W01-D6-rerecord`.
2. Talk for **2 minutes**, unprepared, on this prompt:
   > **"Tell me about the last time you were really late for something."**
   Where were you going? Why were you late? What happened when you got there?
3. Don't stop for the timer to "be perfect". Just keep going for 2 minutes.
4. Count your [3 numbers](../../toolkit/the-3-numbers.md): fillers, long pauses (over 2 seconds), different connectors.
5. Write them in your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md), next to your baseline.

## 4. Live conversation

Read [Live conversations](../../toolkit/live-conversations.md) first (5 minutes), then go.

**Your mission:** have a 15–30 minute conversation with one person (a colleague you don't usually chat with, a language exchange partner, or someone at a meetup). **Tell them one short story** and **listen to one of theirs**.

**Try to:**
1. Tell **one story** in under 2 minutes — your work story, your trip story or your late-for-something story. Use the [4 boxes](day-3-story-structure.md).
2. Use at least **3 connectors** from [Day 1](day-1-storytelling-connectors.md) in it. Write them on your hand if you need to.
3. Ask them for a story: "Has anything like that ever happened to you?"
4. While they talk, use at least **4 different reactions** from [Day 2](day-2-reaction-phrases.md).
5. Describe one person or place with a chunk from [Day 5](day-5-describing-people-and-places.md).

**A first line, ready to use:** *"Hey! So, random question — have you ever had a trip where everything went wrong? Because I've got a good one."*

**Backup plan:** if the conversation falls through, do a 30-minute [voice mode session with Claude](../../toolkit/voice-mode-with-claude.md). Use the standard setup prompt and add: *"Tonight, let's swap stories. Tell me one short story about a bad trip, then ask me for mine. Interrupt me sometimes and react like a real friend would."*

Afterwards, write in your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md): who, how long, which connectors and reactions you used, one moment you got stuck.

## 5. Review and report

1. **Review (10 min):** reread yesterday's 5 phrases out loud. Then scan the whole week's phrase log (about 25 phrases) and star the 5 you used in today's conversation.
2. **Report (10 min):** send Claude your numbers in a normal text chat:

```
Week 1 numbers.
Baseline: [fillers] umms, [pauses] long pauses, [connectors] connectors.
This week's re-record: [fillers] umms, [pauses] long pauses, [connectors] connectors.
Live conversation: [who / how long]. I used these connectors: [...] and these reactions: [...].
My skill check scores: connectors [1–3], reactions [1–3], structure [1–3], past tenses [1–3], description [1–3].
What I found hardest this week: [...]
Can you suggest 3 things to focus on in Week 2 (questions)?
```

**Carry into next week:**
- Pick **4 connectors** you'll use in every Week 2 drill.
- Keep reacting out loud to every clip you shadow.
- Week 2 is all about **questions**. Your stories stay — now you'll ask for other people's.
