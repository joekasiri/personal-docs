> **Tonight is your first monthly check.** Your re-record tonight uses **the same kind of prompt as your baseline**, so you can compare them fairly. You'll do the comparison in section 6.

## Before you start

- Have ready: phone for recording, timer, paper and pen, your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md) with **all** your numbers (baseline + Weeks 1–3), and your baseline recording `W01-D1-baseline`.
- Open [Monthly check](../../toolkit/monthly-check.md) — you'll fill in its results table.
- Confirm your live conversation. If it falls through, use the backup plan in section 4.
- **Don't listen to your baseline yet.** Do the re-record first, unprepared.

## 1. Shadowing (free choice, 10 min)

A short warm-up tonight. Choose the **hardest clip of the week**, or say these, each 3 times:

1. Day 1 — glottal T: "It's really im-POR'nt. I'm not SER'n."
2. Day 2 — held T: "I CAN' go. Who's tha' GUY? Wha' TIME is it?"
3. Day 3 — NT → N: "The IN-er-net is so SLOW. It was like TWEN-ee bucks."
4. Day 4 — dropped T: "Las' night was CRAzy. See ya nex' WEEK."
5. Day 5 — all together: "I jus' fig-ured-i-DOUT, and it's way BEDder now."

## 4. Live conversation

Read [Live conversations](../../toolkit/live-conversations.md) first (5 minutes).

**Your mission:** a 15–30 minute conversation — ideally with a **colleague** or someone who'll ask about your work — where you **talk about your job** and use this week's vocabulary.

**Try to:**
1. Answer "So what do you do?" (or bring it up yourself) with your **3-part job answer** from [Day 5](day-5-talking-about-your-job.md), then ask back.
2. Tell **one short work story** — a recent problem you solved — in under 2 minutes.
3. Use at least **3 phrasal verbs** and **2 collocations** ([Day 1](day-1-phrasal-verbs.md), [Day 2](day-2-collocations.md)).
4. Give one opinion with a **softener** ("It's not that bad, honestly", "I'm not a big fan of…").
5. Use **1–2 idioms** naturally — and end the conversation with "I should hit the road" or "Let's call it a day".

**A first line, ready to use:** *"Hey! How's your week going? Mine's been kinda crazy — we had this weird problem on Tuesday."*

**Backup plan:** a 30-minute [voice mode session with Claude](../../toolkit/voice-mode-with-claude.md). Use the standard setup prompt and add: *"You're someone I just met at a tech meetup. Ask me what I do, ask follow-up questions about my job and a recent problem I solved, and tell me about your own (made-up) job. Interrupt me sometimes, like a real person would. In feedback, also tell me which phrasal verbs, collocations and idioms I used."*

Afterwards, write in your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md): who, how long, which job phrases and idioms you used, one moment you got stuck.

## 5. Review and report

1. **Review (5 min):** reread yesterday's 5 phrases out loud. Scan the whole month's phrase log and count how many phrases have **3 ticks** (used in real conversations) — see [Phrase log](../../toolkit/phrase-log.md#once-a-month).
2. **Report (10 min):** do section 6 first, then send Claude the monthly message at the end of section 6. It replaces the normal weekly message tonight.

**Carry into Month 2:**
- Your three Month 1 stories (work, trip, embarrassing) come back in [Week 5](../../month-02-speed/week-05-better-stories/README.md). Keep the recordings.
- From next week the drill gets faster: **3/2/1** instead of 4/3/2, and shadowing adds **speak-along**.
- Choose your **10 "must use" phrases** for Month 2 from your phrase log — phrases you know but still don't say naturally.

## 6. Monthly check (20 min)

Follow the full procedure in [Monthly check](../../toolkit/monthly-check.md). In short:

1. **Listen to your baseline** (`W01-D1-baseline`), then tonight's recording (`W04-D6-month1-check`). Notice speed, pauses and how natural you sound. Write 3 differences you hear.
2. **Work out your change** — add fillers + long pauses for each recording:
   > (baseline total − tonight's total) ÷ baseline total × 100 = % fewer
   > Example: baseline 20 + 6 = 26; tonight 17 + 5 = 22 → (26 − 22) ÷ 26 × 100 ≈ **15% fewer**.
3. **Compare with the Month 1 targets:**

| Number | Month 1 target | Baseline | Tonight | Target met? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fillers + long pauses | about **15% fewer** than baseline | | | |
| Different connectors in 2 min | **4 or more** | | | |

4. **Fill in the results table** in [Monthly check](../../toolkit/monthly-check.md) (Baseline row and Month 1 row).
5. **If you didn't hit a target**, don't just push on. Read the "If you didn't hit the target" part of [Monthly check](../../toolkit/monthly-check.md), and tell Claude in your message below. Common fixes: redo the Week 1 connector exercises for a week alongside Week 5; record a 2-minute unprepared talk every night (not only on Day 6); or do the [Week 3 time-buyers](../week-03-small-talk/day-4-time-buyers.md) exercises again if your umms are still high.
