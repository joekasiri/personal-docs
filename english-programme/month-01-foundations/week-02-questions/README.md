# Week 2: Questions
[Programme home](../../README.md) › [Month 1: Foundations](../README.md) · [← Week 1](../week-01-storytelling/README.md) · [Week 3 →](../week-03-small-talk/README.md)

## This week's goal

A conversation is not two people taking turns giving speeches. It's questions and answers, going back and forth fast. Many learners freeze not when they *answer*, but when they need to *ask* — because building a question in English means changing the word order, adding "do/did", and getting the tense right, all in half a second. This week you make questions **automatic**: 50 direct questions out loud, follow-up questions that keep people talking, polite indirect questions with the right word order, question tags ("You're from here, right?") and short answers ("Yeah, I did."). On Day 5 you put it all together in a 10-minute self-interview. All week, your shadowing trains the American **question melody** and the reduced starts you hear everywhere: **d'you, didja, whaddya, wouldja, howdja, wherdja**.

## How this builds on last week

In [Week 1](../week-01-storytelling/README.md) you learned to *tell* stories. This week you learn to *ask* for them, and to ask good questions about them. Your 4/3/2 stories continue, but now each one ends with questions to the listener. You'll keep using your [connectors](../week-01-storytelling/day-1-storytelling-connectors.md) and [reactions](../week-01-storytelling/day-2-reaction-phrases.md) — a reaction plus a follow-up question ("No way! How'd that happen?") is one of the most natural combinations in English.

## Day 7: light day

**30 minutes only.** Go for a walk and listen to a **casual interview podcast** — one host asking a guest about their life (search "casual podcast interview", "comedian interview podcast", "life story podcast").

Your listening task:
1. Every time the host asks a **follow-up question**, say it quietly after them ("How come?", "Wait, what happened?").
2. Catch **3 follow-ups** you didn't learn this week. Log them tomorrow.
3. Notice the melody: does the host's voice go **up** or **down** at the end of each question?

Then stop and rest.

- [ ] ask direct questions in past, present, present perfect and future without stopping to build them
