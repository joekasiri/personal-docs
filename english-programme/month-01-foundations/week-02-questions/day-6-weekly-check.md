## 4. Live conversation

Read [Live conversations](../../toolkit/live-conversations.md) first (5 minutes).

**Your mission:** a 15–30 minute conversation with one person where **you ask most of the questions**. Make them do 60% of the talking.

**Try to:**
1. Ask at least **10 questions** in total. Keep a tally on paper or in your head.
2. Ask at least **3 follow-ups** ("How come?", "What was that like?", "Did you end up…?").
3. Use **1 indirect question**: "Do you remember when…?" / "Do you happen to know…?"
4. Use **2 tags**: "You're from around here, right?", "That was fun, wasn't it?"
5. When they ask *you* something, answer with a short answer **plus a detail** ("Yeah, I have! Twice, actually.").

**A first line, ready to use:** *"So how long have you been [working here / living here / doing language exchanges]?"* — then chain from their answer.

**Backup plan:** a 30-minute [voice mode session with Claude](../../toolkit/voice-mode-with-claude.md). Use the standard setup prompt and add: *"Tonight, you're a person I just met at a friend's party. You have an interesting life, but you only give short answers unless I ask good follow-up questions. Make me ask at least 10 questions. Interrupt me sometimes, like a real person would."*

Afterwards, write in your [nightly log](../../toolkit/nightly-log.md): who, how long, how many questions you asked, your best follow-up, one moment you got stuck.

**Carry into next week:**
- Week 3 is **small talk**. Your questions become the engine of every chat.
- Keep chaining: every time someone answers, find the hook.
- From now on, **every 4/3/2 story ends with a question to the listener**.
