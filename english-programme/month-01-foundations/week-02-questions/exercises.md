# Week 2 exercises: Questions
[Week 2 overview](README.md) · [Day 1](day-1-direct-questions.md) · [Day 2](day-2-follow-up-questions.md) · [Day 3](day-3-indirect-questions.md) · [Day 4](day-4-question-tags-and-short-answers.md) · [Day 5](day-5-interview-yourself.md) · [Day 6](day-6-weekly-check.md)

**How to use these exercises:** everything is **out loud**. Questions only become automatic when your mouth has built them many times — reading them silently doesn't count. Use the right melody: wh- questions fall ↘, yes/no questions rise ↗. Record when the exercise says so. Check the answer key *after* you've said your answer.

## Day 1 exercises

### Exercise 1.1: The 50 questions
**Time:** 15 min · **Type:** out loud, timed

1. **Round 1 (6 min):** say every question once, clearly, with the right melody. Notice the helper verb (do / did / have / are / will).
2. **Round 2 (5 min):** say them again, **fast** — no more than 3 seconds per question. Use the reduced forms ("Whaddya…", "Didja…", "Where'd you…").
3. **Round 3 (4 min):** choose 10 questions and **answer each one** in one sentence, as yourself.

**What…**
1. What do you do for a living? *(present simple)*
2. What are you working on right now? *(present continuous)*
3. What did you do last weekend? *(past simple)*
4. What have you been up to lately? *(present perfect)*
5. What are you gonna do this weekend? *(future)*
6. What's your new apartment like? *(be + like)*
7. What happened at the meeting? *(subject question — no "did")*
8. What kind of music are you into? *(preposition at the end)*

**Where…**
9. Where are you from? *(be, preposition at the end)*
10. Where do you live now? *(present simple)*
11. Where did you grow up? *(past simple)*
12. Where have you traveled recently? *(present perfect)*

## Day 2 exercises

### Exercise 2.1: Find the hook
**Time:** 12 min · **Type:** out loud

1. Read each statement out loud as if a friend just said it.
2. Find the **hook** (the most interesting detail).
3. React, then ask **2 different follow-ups** — try to use 2 different types (details, reason, feelings, result). Don't think too long: 30 seconds per statement.
4. Check the model answers, then do the whole list again faster.

1. "I spent the weekend at my parents' place. My dad's building a boat in the garage."
2. "I'm not coming to the office tomorrow. I've got a job interview."
3. "We had to cancel the launch. Again."
4. "I started taking salsa classes."
5. "My neighbor knocked on my door at 2 a.m. last night."
6. "I'm thinking of moving to Lisbon."
7. "I finally finished the redesign. The client hated it."
8. "I ran into my high school teacher at the airport."
9. "I've stopped using social media."

11. "My flight was eleven hours late."
12. "My manager asked me to lead the new project."
13. "I went to a silent retreat. No talking for five days."
14. "I lost my phone on the subway, and someone actually returned it."
15. "I'm learning to code in my free time."

### Exercise 2.2: Podcast pause drill
**Time:** 15 min · **Type:** out loud

1. Play a podcast or interview where someone talks about their life (search "casual podcast interview" or "life story podcast").
2. **Pause** every time the speaker finishes a point — about every 20–30 seconds.
3. Ask **2 follow-up questions** out loud, as if you were the host. Use different types.
4. Don't wait for the perfect question. If nothing comes in 3 seconds, use one of the "always works" follow-ups below.
5. Keep a tally: how many pauses did you do in 15 minutes? Target: **20 pauses = 40 follow-ups**.

**"Always works" follow-ups** (use when you're stuck):
1. How come?
2. Like what?
3. And then what?
4. Wait, what happened?
5. What was that like?
6. How'd that go?
7. What do you mean?
8. Was it worth it?
9. How did you feel about that?
10. Did you end up doing it?
11. Who else was there?
12. What made you do that?

### Exercise 5.2: The 10-minute self-interview
**Time:** 15 min · **Type:** record

1. Choose **one route** below (choose a different one next time).
2. Start recording. Ask the **opening question** out loud, then answer it.
3. **Chain** from your answers. Only use the backup questions if your chain dies.
4. When a topic runs out, switch: *"OK, totally different question —…"* and use the next route's opening question.
5. Don't stop the recording for **10 minutes**. No pause longer than 3 seconds. If you're stuck, ask "How come?" or "Like what?" and answer it.

**Route A: Work**
- Opening: *So what do you actually do all day?*
- Backups: How did you get into this job? · What's the best part? · What's the most annoying part? · Who's the funniest person on your team? · What did you work on last week? · Have you ever made a big mistake at work? · Do you remember your first day? · Where do you see yourself in five years?

**Route B: Home and growing up**
- Opening: *Where did you grow up?*
- Backups: What was it like? · What did you do for fun as a kid? · Who were you closest to in your family? · Were you a good student? · What's something you miss about it? · How often do you go back? · Has it changed a lot? · Would you ever live there again?
